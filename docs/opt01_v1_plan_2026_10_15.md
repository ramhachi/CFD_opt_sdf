## Decisions to register (D1–D3), each a new content-addressed record

- **D1 Backend supersession for the first OPT-01 step (#25).** The update direction of OPT-01 v1 is computed from dual-grid centered φ-space secants
  over a registered basis of at most 8 directions (cost 2k+1 runs per grid), with the LOWDIM-04 L1-robust cone solver. This supersedes #25's
  "production backend" dependency for OPT-01 v1 only; #25 stays open for any high-dimensional backend, and FD stays verification-only beyond 8
  directions. No gradient is claimed: the secants are finite-step responses, not derivatives.
- **D2 GEOM-02 gate profile v2.** `near_zero_gradient_band` is redefined from "central |∇φ| ≥ 0.5 at interior sign-change nodes" to
  "**at every interior sign-change node, the largest one-sided slope magnitude |Δφ|/h over the six neighbours is ≥ 0.5**", i.e. the zero level is
  locally resolvable from at least one side. Rationale: a kink (one-sided slopes ±1, central 0) has an accurate distance and a well-defined
  crossing; a degeneracy (all one-sided slopes small: exact-zero ties, flat plateaus) has an ill-conditioned crossing location. The central-difference
  counts stay reported. A φ-space update never uses the normal velocity −δφ/|∇φ|; any future normal-velocity backend must re-register the central
  rule as hard. All other gates and thresholds unchanged (new profile hash). **Non-blind:** the #29 diagnostic shows the parent's 154 failing nodes
  all have a one-sided slope ≥ 1.0 and the baseline's include 25 tie-plateau nodes; the expected outcome is "parent passes, baseline fails" and is
  recorded as a prediction.
- **D3 OPT-01 v1 update contract.** Topology-fixed (`allowed_events=()`), no reinitialization, P22-v2 `N_phi ≤ 8272`, GEOM-02 profile v2 all gates
  hard, dual-grid acceptance (strictly more than 3e-5 N downforce gain and computed drag change ≤ 0 on both flow_24 and flow_32), step ladder with
  the LOWDIM-04B selection rule, rollback = keep the parent; lineage = parent and trial state hashes plus the registered contract hashes.

2. **Retroactive conformance (descriptive, labelled):** does the LOWDIM-04B trial satisfy D3? Its acceptance was decided before D2/D3 existed, so

3. **Prospective OPT-01 v1 trial (Kaggle, preregistered):** from the LOWDIM-04B accepted state (a new parent): Stage A secants (9 states per grid,
   18 runs) → cone direction → Stage B ladder (14 runs) under D3, everything frozen before submission (reuse the LOWDIM-04A/B scripts by parameterizing
   the parent; no new solver code). Any unambiguous ACCEPT or REJECT closes #30 (its Done condition); NO_DIRECTION is also unambiguous. This is the second
   iterate of the LOWDIM lineage, run for contract conformance, not for gain (the LOWDIM exit rule's "no third cycle on gain headroom alone" stands).
