## 1. Questions and claim ceiling
- **Q1 (sigma_d).** The placement-phase SD of same-phase paired differences `Delta_{g,m,k} = F_g(m, k) - F_g(parent, k)` (F = downforce, drag; grid g; state m; phase k) on flow_24, flow_32 and flow_48, and how it scales with the size of the difference (σ_d(Δ), §5.7).
- **Q2 (support, R2), descriptive.** The relative-error ordering `r = sigma_d / |mean_k Delta|` of the wide-support offset D0 and the small-support Gaussian offset s050 on flow_32, with the wide analytic arms between. R2 is already adopted; no decision depends on Q2. The ordering confounds support with response size, sign and volume (§3) and does not test whether relative error depends on support as such.
- **Q3 (optimisation grid, R3).** The ratio of the phase-mean difference of L+ on flow_32 to flow_48, with an interval and an equivalence label; the same for flow_24 against flow_32 (does the GRID-02 flow_24 outlier persist on v18?).
- **Q4 (E2 and M3a inputs).** S_b, S_c of the E2 rule, the paired analogue S_d, OpenFOAM retention of each difference, and the coefficient tables of §5.7 that the M3a (#62) registration takes.

M2 does not optimise, accept a shape or set an M3a threshold; it fixes numbers. Claim ceiling: the in-plane (x, y) placement-phase spread of Re = 80 reduced-laminar WaterLily differences, and the local (±4 mm, 3-axis) shift spread of coarse-mesh OpenFOAM differences, for the registered states of one analytic parent at one amplitude each, uncertified geometry. Not: physical downforce, grid convergence, a gradient, a σ_d for other modes, amplitudes or parents (σ_d(Δ) is a descriptive fit over seven states), the ride-height (z) phase.

**Selection rule (fixed before U1, `analyze_unc01.py u1`):** score(m) = `|F32(m+, k0) - F32(m-, k0)|` (downforce, flow_32, native phase). **L** = argmax over the candidates; **L2** = argmax over the candidates of a family other than L's; ties go to the order pitch, camber, reflex, twist, sweep. The native phase is the cell-aligned placement, an atypical one; U2 does not use it. There is no selection without `U0_PASS`, which needs all three U01 kernels verified and therefore every U1 row; no default L.
Q2 confounds: D0 is outward and changes the smoothed volume by +7.6 %, s050 is inward and nearly volume-neutral; amplitudes 3.0 vs 4.4 mm; support 1.0 vs 0.02.
The roll: on flow_32 the flow cell equals the SDF cell and both grids' origins differ by whole cells, so `roll` at phase k is the parent at phase k translated by exactly one cell: the discretisation is identical and `Delta_roll` is the finite-box effect of a 25 mm downstream move (expected small and nearly phase-independent). It is the clean x-translation negative control #62 asks for. The draft's surge ±31.1 mm (1.24 cells) is dropped: its difference measures the parent's own placement roughness at a 0.24-cell offset, which σ_F of the parent already measures (strategy review S1).

## 4. U2 design
- **Phases.** Phase k moves `W4_CANONICAL_ORIGIN_M` by `(f_kx, f_ky, 0) h_g` (the origin string is rounded to 1e-10 m, within 5e-11 m of the exact value, and round-trips exactly through the float parsers of the job and the verifier), `f_k = frac(k (0.7548776662, 0.5698402910))` (R2 low-discrepancy sequence), `h_g` = 1/30, 1/40, 1/60 m; z never moves. **U2 uses k = 1..6 on flow_24 and flow_32 and k = 1..4 on flow_48**, disjoint from U1's native k = 0, so the L selection uses no U2 data (the U2 values are still values of the same smooth function of the phase, and the rule deliberately picks a large responder: r of L is not a typical r). All states of a grid share its phases (R4). On flow_24 and flow_48 the SDF lattice (25 mm) is not the flow cell, so the (flow grid, SDF lattice) offset repeats over 3-4 flow cells; the unit torus of f is a parametrisation of the flow-grid phase only.
- **States.** flow_32: parent, D0, L+, L-, L_mid (or L2-), L2+, s050, roll (8 x 6 = 48 runs); flow_24 (demoted, R3; for the outlier question): parent, D0, L+ (3 x 6 = 18); flow_48: parent, L+ (2 x 4 = 8; #61's "parent and the best mode").

## 5. Rules (computed by the frozen `scripts/analyze_unc01.py`)
### 5.1 U0 gate (engineering)
On the U01 smoke output (flow_24) before any formal U01 push, and then on `v18__parent__k0` of U01 on flow_24, flow_32, flow_48: harness `verify` passes (`verify --smoke` for the smoke); `finite_u`, `finite_p`, `finite_forces` are true in the job summary; the relative half-window drift `|m(80,100) - m(100,120)| / |m(80,120)|`, with `m(a,b)` the time-weighted (trapezoidal) mean of the force CSV column clipped to [a, b] tU/L with linear interpolation at the ends (`fd08_v2_campaign_io.clipped_window` / `time_weighted_mean`), is <= 0.02 for drag and for downforce (the existing W3/W4 rule); downforce > 0; and, as a gross-error check (wrong phi or origin, not physics), `0.5 <= F_v18 / F_v17 <= 2` against the native v17 parents (flow_24 0.33188 N, flow_32 0.36178 N: OPT-01 Stage B / JOB-02; flow_48 0.36435 N: GRID-02). Any failure: `U0_FAIL` (physical gate). A native parent row missing because its kernel has no verified table is `U0_INCOMPLETE`: the kernel is handled as `INFRA_LOSS` (§8), and `U0_INCOMPLETE` after that resubmission stops the study. The kernel outputs read here (summary, CSV) must have the SHA-256 the verified table recorded.

### 5.2 U2 estimators (grid g, state m ≠ parent, response r ∈ {downforce, drag}, the P phases of the grid)
- `Delta_bar = mean_k Delta`, `sigma_d` = sample SD (P - 1 dof), `SE = sigma_d / sqrt(P)`, `t = scipy.stats.t.ppf(0.95, P - 1)` (2.015 for P = 6, 2.353 for P = 4).
- Intervals: `sigma_d` 90 %: `[sigma_d sqrt((P-1)/chi2.ppf(0.95, P-1)), sigma_d sqrt((P-1)/chi2.ppf(0.05, P-1))]` (factors 0.672-2.089 for P = 6, 0.620-2.920 for P = 4); `Delta_bar` 90 %: `Delta_bar ± t SE`.
- **resolved** iff `|Delta_bar| > t SE` (the 90 % interval excludes 0; equivalently `r < sqrt(P)/t`, 1.22 for P = 6 and 0.85 for P = 4). `above_3se` (`|Delta_bar| > 3 SE`, the M3a "3 sigma" form) is reported beside it.
- `r = sigma_d / |Delta_bar|`; `r_lo = sigma_lo / (|Delta_bar| + t SE)`, `r_hi = sigma_hi / (|Delta_bar| - t SE)` (`inf` when unresolved). The interval joins two 90 % intervals: coverage is at least about 80 %, labelled so.
- Descriptive: the parent's phase SD `sigma_F`; `sigma_d / (sqrt(2) sigma_F)`; the correlation of `Delta_m(k)` with `F(parent, k)` and between states (a control-variate hint for M3b); each run's drift, flagged when > 0.002 (all runs are retained; a non-finite run fails `verify`); for L on flow_32: the even part `(Delta(L+) + Delta(L-)) / 2` per phase, its mean and SD; for each U1 state also measured in U2 on flow_32: `(Delta_k0 - Delta_bar) / sigma_d` (how atypical the native phase is).
- Phases: a deterministic R2 set treated as exchangeable samples. A check by simulation with random smooth periodic responses (harmonics up to 2) gave 90 % coverage 0.92-0.95 for sigma_d and 0.92-0.98 for the mean; it is not validated for discontinuous (cell-crossing) phase dependence.
- Minimum data: flow_32 needs at least two of f32a/b/c verified (P >= 4, `P_reduced` recorded); flow_24 needs its kernel; flow_48 needs both kernels. Otherwise the grid is `INCOMPLETE` and every quantity that needs it reads `INCOMPLETE`.

### 5.3 Q2 (descriptive ordering; downforce and drag reported)
On flow_32: both D0 and s050 resolved: `D0_LOWER_R` if `r_hi(D0) < r_lo(s050)`, `S050_LOWER_R` if `r_hi(s050) < r_lo(D0)`, else `NOT_SEPARATED`; D0 resolved and s050 not: `S050_UNRESOLVED`; D0 unresolved: `D0_UNRESOLVED`. Printed with every state's r and interval, `sigma_d / sigma_F`, sigma_d per mm of maximum change, and the §3 confounds. Expected before the data (numerical review N1): with P = 6 an unresolved s050 is likely, and `D0_LOWER_R` needs r(D0) below about 0.3. `Q2_OF`: the same on U3 (§5.6), not comparable with the WaterLily ordering (different shift ensemble). flow_24 is not used.

### 5.4 Q3 (downforce registered, drag reported)
For two grids a (tested) and b (reference) and one state: `UNRESOLVED` unless both differences are resolved; `DIFFERENT` if their signs differ; otherwise `R = Delta_bar_a / Delta_bar_b` with the 90 % interval `exp(log R ± t' sqrt((SE_a / Delta_bar_a)^2 + (SE_b / Delta_bar_b)^2))`, `t' = t.ppf(0.95, min(P_a, P_b) - 1)`: `EQUIVALENT` if the interval lies inside [0.8, 1.25], `DIFFERENT` if it lies entirely outside, else `INDETERMINATE`. R and its interval are the graded output M3a uses.
- Main: L+, flow_32 (a) vs flow_48 (b).
- flow_24 vs flow_32 (reference) for L+ and D0 (L+ only with f24r): `FLOW24_DIFFERENT` (the outlier persists) if any is `DIFFERENT`; `FLOW24_EQUIVALENT` if all are `EQUIVALENT`; `FLOW24_UNRESOLVED` if any is `UNRESOLVED` and none is `DIFFERENT`; else `FLOW24_INDETERMINATE`.
- Operating characteristics (simulation, equal relative noise r on both grids, P = 6 and 4): true ratio 1: `EQUIVALENT` 1.00 / 0.69 / 0.20 / 0.04 at r = 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.15 / 0.2, `INDETERMINATE` above; `UNRESOLVED` 0.10 at r = 0.5 and 0.69 at r = 1. True ratio 1.3: `DIFFERENT` 0.19 at r = 0.05, below 0.08 for r >= 0.1. With GRID-02-like noise the expected label is `INDETERMINATE`; the decisive part is then R with its interval. The band [0.8, 1.25] is the registrant's R3 tolerance (GRID-02 flow_32/flow_48 slope ratio 1.007 on v17). Consequence: `EQUIVALENT` supports R3 for large modes; `DIFFERENT` sends M3a to flow_48 confirmations for every design; `INDETERMINATE`/`UNRESOLVED` leave R3 as the working rule and M3a must carry the interval of R.

### 5.5 Q4: E2 inputs
E2 (`docs/architecture_effectiveness_2026_09.md` §9, lines 172-180): `I_f = (J_b,f - J_c,f) / max(|J_b,f|, J_scale)`, `S_b = |J_b,f - J_b,m| / max(|J_b,f|, J_scale)`, `S_c = |J_c,f - J_c,m| / max(...)`, Go if `I_f > 2 max(S_b, S_c)`. Here f = flow_48, m = flow_32, b = parent, c = L+, J = the phase mean on the **common phases** (k present on both grids, 1..4) with J = -downforce or drag; `J_scale = 3.6e-3 N` for both (1 % of the v17 flow_48 parent downforce 0.36435 N, GRID-02; fixed before any v18 number; binds only if |J_b,48| < 3.6e-3 N).
Recorded: `S_b`, `S_c`, `S_d = |Delta_bar_48 - Delta_bar_32| / max(|J_b,48|, J_scale)` (common phases), each with its SE from the per-phase values (`S_d`: `sqrt(SE_32^2 + SE_48^2) / denominator`); `I_f(L+)` (descriptive; L is a selected large responder, so it is biased high); the implied minimum gains `2 max(S_b, S_c)` (literal) and `2 S_d` (paired); the all-phase versions descriptively. Stated before the data: |·| of a noisy difference is biased upward (about 0.8 SE under equality), and S_b, S_c are expected to be dominated by the parent's placement noise (GRID-02: a half-cell shift moved the v17 flow_32 parent by 9.6 %), so the literal E2 rule may be unreachable for M3a-size gains; which form M3a uses is for the M3a registration.

### 5.6 U3 (OpenFOAM)
For each U2 flow_32 state m and the 16 mirror pairs (32 shifts): `Delta_OF(m, j) = F_OF(m, j) - F_OF(parent, j)`; the pair mean of the two mirror shifts is the unit (16 pair means, 15 dof): `Delta_bar_OF` = mean of the pair means, `SE_OF` = their SD / sqrt(16 or fewer), `sigma_d,OF` reported as the SD of the 32 shift differences and of the pair means; resolution as §5.2 with the pair dof. A failed case removes its shift **and its mirror** for every state (matched pairs, whole pairs only); fewer than 12 whole pairs: U3 `INCOMPLETE`. A state not run because its STL failed the export gate is `NOT_MEASURED`.
Retention `rho_m = Delta_bar_OF / Delta_bar_32` (WaterLily flow_32 U2), when both are resolved, with the interval of |rho| `exp(log|rho| ± t' sqrt((SE_OF/Delta_OF)^2 + (SE_32/Delta_32)^2))`, `t' = t.ppf(0.95, min(P_32, P_OF) - 1)` (5 with the full flow_32 phase set); sign agreement reported separately. Limits stated with every U3 number: the OpenFOAM shifts are local (0.5-4 mm on each axis including z, about 10 % of a 25-50 mm surface cell), the WaterLily phases span a whole flow cell in x and y; so sigma_d,OF and Q2_OF are not the WaterLily ensemble, and rho compares a local OpenFOAM mean with a cell-averaged WaterLily mean; the STL extraction (r = 8 marching cubes, sub-cell components removed) is a geometry-representation difference between the two.

### 5.7 M3a coefficient tables (printed; M3a decides what it adopts)
For downforce and drag: (a) per state and grid: sigma_d with its interval, r with its interval, `|Delta_bar| / SE`, `above_3se`; (b) relative noise for planning: per wide arm (D0, L+, L-, L_mid, L2+) `phases_needed(r) = ceil(9 r^2)` (phases for `|Delta| > 3 SE`) at the point r and at r_hi, and the largest r_hi over those arms; (c) the roll's sigma_d and Delta_bar on flow_32 (the paired-difference floor when the discretisation is identical); (d) σ_d(Δ): OLS of log sigma_d on log |Delta_bar| over the flow_32 non-control states with |Delta_bar| > 0 (slope near 0: additive noise; near 1: multiplicative), with its standard errors; (e) Q3's R with its interval (grid spread); (f) retention rho per state with its interval; (g) S_b, S_c, S_d with SEs. The roll is not a wide arm. No absolute-Δ phase table is printed (it would assume sigma_d independent of Δ). These numbers hold for the measured states and amplitudes only.

### 5.8 Overall M2 outcome
`M2_COMPLETE`: flow_32 with P = 6, flow_48 complete, flow_24 measured (or `NOT_MEASURED` by the §4 budget rule), U3 `COMPLETE`. `M2_PARTIAL`: flow_32 estimable (P >= 4) and anything else missing; the record lists the missing deliverables. `M2_INCOMPLETE`: flow_32 not estimable. M3a registers from `M2_COMPLETE`, or names the partial deliverables it uses.

## 8. Freeze, re-run, amendment and stopping rules (fail-closed)
- Order: plan rev. 1 → three-angle plan review → rev. 2 (§12) → one source review of the scripts and tests → **freeze 1** (committed and pushed, #61 comment) → U01 smoke → U0 smoke check → U01 build and harness prerun freeze (**freeze 1b**) → U01 formal kernels → U0 gate → U1 selection → U2 campaign build, U2 smoke, U2 harness prerun freeze, U3 inputs and U3 development smoke → **freeze 2** (committed and pushed, #61 comment) → all U2 kernels and U3, unconditionally → one analysis.
