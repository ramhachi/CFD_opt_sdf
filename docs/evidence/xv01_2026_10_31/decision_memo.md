# XV-01 (#65) registration — decision memo, revision 1 (2026-11-01)

Companion to `docs/xv01_registration_2026_10_31.md` (revision 1, `DRAFT_REVIEWED_REV1`). Revision 0 of this memo (commit `02a74312` / `81826220`) recorded the first decisions; the plan review (`plan_review/{numerical,governance,strategy}.md`, decisions in `plan_review/adoption.md`) changed several of them. Each decision gives the alternatives, the choice, the reason and the residual risk, with the review findings that drove a change. Nothing here is a new measurement; no OpenFOAM force of the finalist exists. Decision-maker: the registrant under the 2026-10-13 delegation; every choice also goes through the one source review before freeze.

## D1. What XV verifies, and with which run set
**Alternatives.** (a) the preparation note's option A (V1 16 pairs, V2/V3 2 pairs each; 80 cases); (b) option A′ (V1 16, V2/V3 4 pairs each; 96 cases); (c) A′ plus V3 at more pairs; (d) a staged design (V1/V2 first, V3 only if V1/V2 do not already decide FAIL); (e) V1/V2 only.
**Choice: (b), plus cheap V1 controls and an extension panel (rev. 1: 481 cases, about 170 CPU h).** A gives dof 1 on V2 and V3 (t_0.95 = 6.31); the ablation gives P(PASS) 0.06 against 0.17, mostly through `UNRESOLVED`. A′ gives dof 3 at about 149 CPU h of V3; a fifth or sixth V3 pair adds 37-75 CPU h for t 2.13-2.02 instead of 2.35. (d) saves 0.16-0.25 x 149 CPU h in expectation (the probability of a resolved FAIL on the V1/V2 rules, numerical review) but loses the V3 drag and consistency measurement exactly on the FAIL branch, which is the information M4 most needs (strategy S4 agrees). (e) drops the finest mesh where the camber behaviour is least known. Rev. 1 adds, on V1 only, the six SCREEN-measured states in the camber extension (strategy S7(b), limited): about 5.6 CPU h for the only OpenFOAM evidence on the extension besides the finalist.
**Residual risk.** dof 3 on V2/V3: a true value near a threshold stays `UNRESOLVED` more often than with more pairs; V3 has one pair of slack.

## D2. Single-finalist uncertainty (requirements §2.1)
**Alternatives.** (a) copy FID's V2 pooled SD and the V3 leave-one-out regression residual (1.45e-3 N); (b) own pair ensembles per level with no floor; (c) own ensembles with a floor from an OpenFOAM-internal placement SD; (d) a calibration/prediction term inside the SE.
**Choice: (c)**, floor = the FID V2 pooled pair SD (downforce 1.98e-4 N, drag 1.37e-4 N, dof 12) on V2 and V3; V1 unchanged from FID (own 16 pairs, no floor). (a) is excluded by the requirements note and the FID handoff; the numerical review adds that the 1.45e-3 N residual is driven by one design (5.3e-4 N without camber -19.7). (b) can produce a spuriously small SE from 4 units of a deterministic solver. (d) mixes a prediction uncertainty into a measurement rule. The numerical review found the floor a plausible V3 scale (FID's V2 single-shift SD 4.2e-4 N), neither clearly conservative nor anti-conservative; on the V3 consistency margins it practically never binds (kept as an SE = 0 guard).
**Residual risk.** The Halton panel is not an iid sample; pairs 81-84 are sign-imbalanced (disclosed, registration §5.1).

## D3. Normalisation of the OpenFOAM floor
**Alternatives.** (a) `Delta_L / J_b,48`; (b) `Delta_L / |F_parent,L|`; (c) `max` of the two.
**Choice (rev. 1): (a), frozen as `x_OF = 3.3687409403100554e-3 N`** on every level. Revision 0's `max` was inert (every FID OpenFOAM parent downforce is 0.20-0.21 N < 0.266 N) and its "mean parent downforce over the valid XV cases" was ambiguous (numerical N8). The rationale is corrected: the active term of `g_OF` is `S_d,OF`, which is normalised by the OpenFOAM parent; XV takes `J_b,48` because it is SCREEN's normaliser and the basis of SCREEN §5.7's projection (`% of J_b,48` against 1.27 %), and it is the stricter reading by 1.25-1.32x. (b) is reported beside it.
**Residual risk.** None for the outcome at the projected values (ablation: 0.17 vs 0.17).

## D4. Drag constraint on the OpenFOAM arm
**Alternatives.** (a) SCREEN's point sign `D <= 0`; (b) resolved non-increase on V1 only; (c) resolved non-increase on every level, any resolved violation a FAIL, otherwise UNRESOLVED; (d) a drag allowance or non-inferiority margin (strategy S1: 5.4e-4 N, the finalist's SCREEN drag SE_eff).
**Choice: (c), with its meaning stated and a materiality label.** The requirements note re-evaluates "the registered hard constraints" — SCREEN's drag non-increase with allowance 0 — "with propagated uncertainty", keeps anything unresolved out of PASS and reserves FAIL for resolved violations; a sign that depends on the mesh is not a confirmed constraint. (d) would replace the registered constraint by "increase <= 5.4e-4 N"; that is a change of the problem, not a re-evaluation, and the margin's source (a WaterLily measurement SE) is a resolution, not a physical tolerance. Rejected as a rule; adopted as the label `DRAG_MATERIALITY` read by the consequence map (§8.1), which is the strategy reviewer's stated minimum alternative. Ablations: P(PASS) 0.17 (registered) vs 0.22 (point sign), 0.39 (V1 only), 0.32 (margin).
**Meaning (numerical N4).** PASS needs the one-sided upper bound <= 0: a design with zero true drag change cannot pass; at the floor the effective thresholds are ± 1.62e-4 N (0.05 % of the parent drag); P(PASS) is 0.17 at a common true change of -2e-4 N and 0.91 at -4e-4 N.
**Residual risk.** The V3 drag projects at about -1e-4 N, within model uncertainty (reflex drag retention x 0.8 / 1.2 moves its pass probability from 0.10 to 0.85). In a third of the reference replicates the drag rule is not passed while every upper bound is below the L1 drag resolution; §8.1 reads that as "not confirmed at OpenFOAM resolution", not as a material violation.

## D5. V3 consistency for one finalist
**Alternatives.** (a) drop the set rule; (b) one-sided (lower only); (c) two-sided point-pass with resolved departures on both sides FAIL (rev. 0); (d) require the whole interval inside the band; (e) two-sided point-pass, a resolved lower departure FAIL, a resolved upper departure UNRESOLVED.
**Choice (rev. 1): (e).** (a) is a silent V3 drop (requirements §2.2). (b) loosens the inherited two-sided band after the FID values were visible (ablation: P(PASS) 0.17 → 0.23; governance). (d) is stricter than FID's point-pass (0.12). Between (c) and (e) the requirements' own text decides: FAIL is "a resolved constraint violation or a boundedly negative verified decision" and a "V3 conflict" is its example of UNRESOLVED; a V3 gain resolved above V1 / 0.6 is neither a violation nor negative for the improvement, while V3 resolved below 0.6 V1 is boundedly negative for its mesh-robustness. P(PASS) is the same under (c) and (e) (numerical N5): only the classification differs. The constant is exactly 1/0.6, and the rule requires a positive common-pair V1 mean.
**Residual risk.** A camber-dominated finalist projects V3/V1 of about 1.2-1.9 (FID camber -19.7: 1.52); P(upper point-pass) 0.68 in the reference scenario, 0.35 if only camber -19.7 is the source.

## D6. EX, REF, M, R-best and the extension panel
EX is outcome-relevant for the finalist (#65 scope; FID's insensitivity was shown for two other shapes; FID values 6.0e-6 N against 8.0e-4 N and 3.1e-7 against 9.8e-4 N). **REF (W4) is a chain control in rev. 1** (governance G13): it is the only control that exercises shape → STL → mesh → force on new shifts (NC is a translation, the replays and the determinism repeat are parent-only); `NOT_CONSISTENT` is INVALID, `MISSING` keeps PASS out. M and R-best are descriptive comparisons with a power note (about 2.0e-3 and 3.2e-3 N expected OpenFOAM advantages against a 2-3e-4 N pair-difference SE). The extension panel (six SCREEN states with u_camber < -1) is descriptive (`EXT_V1_RETENTION`). Rejected (strategy S7): the component states at the finalist's amplitudes (no WaterLily values, so no retention) and the full SCREEN panel (a calibration study of its own for M4).

## D7. Funding on a low stated pass probability
**Alternatives.** (a) fund the full run set; (b) do not fund; (c) fund a reduced set; (d) loosen rules to raise the probability; (e) decide funding after the smokes (rev. 0).
**Choice (rev. 1): (a), recorded in freeze 1 before any XV case** (governance G2, strategy S4). The composed-gate pass probability projects at about 0.17 (0.00-0.35 over structural scenarios, model uncertainty at least a factor 2); §5.7 alone suggested 0.80-1.00 for the gain floor and 0.30-0.94 for the binding G4 bound. The cost is local CPU (about 170 CPU h plus at most 40 CPU h of smokes and the probe), no spending. The non-PASS outcomes answer M4's constraint-transfer and extension-fidelity questions, which no other registered study answers. (e) would let the decision depend on finalist statuses (a discretionary exit after the first XV cases); after freeze 1 only mechanical stops apply. (b) leaves L2 empty and #66 without a listed M3 record. (c) would not measure V3. (d) rejected (D3-D5). The probability is disclosed and is not a criterion.
**Residual risk.** About two days of the shared host; a FAIL is the more likely outcome and is reported as a decision with its cause class (§8.1), not tuned.

## D8. No extension round, one re-registration at most
FID had a registered extension for `UNRESOLVED_STAT`; SCREEN had none. XV has one fixed run set: an unresolved rule is `XV01_UNRESOLVED` (strategy S8, option ii). At most one re-registration in total for #65, shared by every pre-run exit and the non-FAIL non-PASS outcomes, none after FAIL; it needs an explicit decision recorded on #65, the same finalist, fresh Halton >= 101, no pooling and rules no weaker (defined in §8.2, with what it can and cannot change). Reason: an extension triggered by which rule is unresolved is a result-guided addition; dof 3 was chosen up front (D1).

## D9. Outcome order and mechanical stops (rev. 1)
**Choice.** INVALID (a judged chain control fails) → INCOMPLETE (any registered formal kernel lost or not run, whatever else) → UNRESOLVED (a chain control not judged) → FAIL → PASS → UNRESOLVED (governance G1). A FAIL therefore needs a completed run set and judged controls, as the requirements define it. INCOMPLETE is simply "any lost or not-run kernel": simpler and stricter than a counterfactual test of whether the lost cases could have mattered. The runner's statuses are mapped to physical results or infrastructure losses by a frozen table (G3); the budget has a hard cap, a freeze-2 limit, pair-complete V3 waves and bounded smoke/probe attempts (G4); every pre-run stop has a terminal record (G2).

## D10. What is not done
No WaterLily case (SCREEN owns that arm). No other SCREEN candidate verified in place of the finalist. No OpenFOAM mesh beyond V1/V2/V3, no GCI or grid-convergence claim. No high-Re, no FSAE, no flag change. No automatic branch from any cause (`XV_WL_OF` does not trigger FID's smooth-advection study); a research NO-GO is only an explicit recorded M4 decision citing the consequence map.
