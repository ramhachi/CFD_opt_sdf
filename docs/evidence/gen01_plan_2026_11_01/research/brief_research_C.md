# Brief C: full-vehicle RANS validity, validation data, and unsteady gradients (web research)

Use WebSearch (mode "extended" where results are thin) and WebFetch. Do not write files; return a report (~2000 words, tables preferred, every claim cited: DOI/venue/year or URL + access date). Today is 2026-11-01. Say "unverified" where you cannot verify.

Context: a research project wants a generic whole-vehicle (FSAE race car) aerodynamic optimizer driven by adjoint steady RANS. Known problems: at low ride height the ground-effect wing's steady RANS does not converge (the project's own 2D section study at Re_c 4.6e5 at h/c 0.134 and 0.09; Vogt, Barber & Leonardi 2007 reported the same), peak downforce height is turbulence-model dependent, and the only public primary data (Zerihan & Zhang 2000, Tyrrell 026) are limited to qualitative trends by their authors. The project needs to know how much of an FSAE car is in a "steady, gradient-friendly" regime, what validation data exist, and what to do where the flow is unsteady.

Questions:
1. Full-car RANS fidelity for race cars / open-wheel cars: which regions converge in steady RANS (SST, SA, realizable k-ε), which are inherently unsteady (wheel wakes, diffuser, rear wing wake, front wing in ground effect at low h/c), typical steady-RANS vs wind-tunnel errors in downforce/drag/balance for FSAE and open-wheel cars (Wordley & Saunders; Craig & Passmore 2014; Pacheco, Marta, Eça 2024; any 2020-2026 FSAE CFD validation papers; DrivAer benchmark RANS/DES errors as reference), mesh sizes used (cells), y+ strategies, wheel-rotation (MRF/sliding/rotating wall) and moving-ground treatments.
2. Published high-quality validation data for ground-effect wings and open-wheel cars (beyond Zerihan & Zhang): Cranfield series (Correia 2014; Roberts 2016-2019), Zhou, Overmars & Westerweel 2026 (if it exists: Tyrrell 026 new data), Pegrum 2006 (wheel-wing), Mahon & Zhang, Heyder-Bruckner 2011 (wing-wheel), MIRA/DrivAer, any FSAE wind-tunnel datasets with uncertainty, and whether any are open data.
3. Gradients in unsteady regimes: unsteady adjoint of URANS/DES windowed averages (cost, memory, checkpointing), chaotic sensitivity limits (Lea, Allen, Haine 2000; Wang 2014 LSS; Ni & Wang NILSS 2017; Blonigan), ensemble/finite-difference sensitivities with common random numbers, "steady adjoint of a time-averaged field" heuristics and their documented errors, and pseudo-transient / LTS steady solutions as a differentiable surrogate for low-ride-height wings.
4. Experimental-validation strategy for an optimized FSAE car: on-track coast-down / constant-speed methods, pitot/pressure taps, strain-gauge pushrod loads, wind-tunnel scale-model costs, and how uncertainties compare to typical optimization gains (1-5 %).
5. Rules/packaging: 2025-2026 FSAE (FSAE Japan, FSG, FSAE-A) aerodynamic device rules (envelope boxes, radius limits, ground clearance), so packaging constraints can be expressed as SDF envelope constraints.

Finish with a one-page recommendation: which vehicle regions a steady-RANS adjoint can credibly optimise, the validation ladder (2D section → wing+wheel → full car), the data to use at each rung, and the top 5 risks.
