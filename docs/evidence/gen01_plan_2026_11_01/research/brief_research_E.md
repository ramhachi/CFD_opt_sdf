# Brief E: WaterLily-native, topology-free, GPU optimisation at moderate Re (web research)

Use WebSearch (mode "extended" where results are thin) and WebFetch. Do not write files; return a report (~1800 words, tables preferred, every claim cited: DOI/venue/year or URL + access date). Today is 2026-11-01. Say "unverified" where you cannot verify; say "read" when you read the primary text.

Context: an FSAE aerodynamics research project rebuilt its optimiser on WaterLily.jl (Julia, GPU, uniform Cartesian grid, BDIM immersed boundary, implicit LES, ~131-135 bytes per cell, WaterLily 1.8.0 pinned, Kaggle T4) because OpenFOAM/DAFoam were too heavy. The geometry is a signed distance field (SDF) on the flow grid. Forward-mode AD of WaterLily failed (tangent grows exponentially from a far-field corner even at Re 80, where the finite response decays; Enzyme reverse-mode stopped at a MethodError). The project now wants a GENERIC, topology-free optimiser (e.g. a front wing whose number of elements is not fixed in advance) built on WaterLily at moderate Re (Re_c 1e3-1e4), with gradient-free or reduced-basis finite-difference gradients for now, and OpenFOAM primal runs (no adjoint) only as the high-Re verifier on a 128 GB machine. The candidate representation is a union of analytic component SDFs (morphable plates/airfoils with position, chord, camber, thickness, incidence) plus fixed mesh-SDFs (body, wheels, ground), in the spirit of Moving Morphable Components (MMC).

Questions:
1. **Explicit/component-based topology optimisation**: Guo, Zhang, Zhong (2014) MMC; MMV (moving morphable voids); topology description functions built from component SDFs (union via max/min or smooth approximations, e.g. R-functions, KS); component vanishing/overlap rules; applications in fluids (Stokes/Navier-Stokes MMC papers 2019-2026); any aerodynamic multi-element use. What gradient-free or FD-based variants exist, and typical variable counts.
2. **WaterLily.jl status 2026**: latest release (v1.10.0 2026-10-21 and later), new features relevant here (multi-GPU, non-uniform/stretched grids, AMR, wall models, moving bodies, force integration, Float64 on GPU, 2D mode), measured throughput (cell-steps per second) on T4 / A100 / RTX 4090-5090 if published, the Enzyme reverse-mode PR #285 and issue #358 ("AD derivative noise on Float32") status, and the Lauber-Weymouth-Limbert BDIM-σ for thin bodies. Validation range of WaterLily's iLES (TGV, sphere Re 3700, cylinder, flapping foils; any airfoil at Re 1e4-1e5).
3. **Stabilising tangents/adjoints of time-marching maps whose nonlinear iteration converges but whose linearisation is unstable**: Campobasso & Giles 2004 (RPM for linear flow solvers), Skamagkis et al. 2021 (RPM on primal and adjoint), Christianson 1994, Krakos & Darmofal 2010; for chaotic cases LSS/NILSS cost; any work on AD of immersed-boundary solvers (IBAMR, Nek, lettuce/LBM) and how they handle the Poisson solve (IFT vs unrolling). Is "adjoint of the steady fixed point via Newton-Krylov on the time-stepping residual" a known practical route for pressure-projection codes?
4. **Gradient-free / reduced-basis optimisation with noisy GPU evaluations at 20-60 variables**: CMA-ES budgets, DFO-LS/Py-BOBYQA with restarts, Bayesian optimisation (TuRBO) in 30-60 D, SPSA, and FD in a Laplace-Beltrami or PCA basis; evaluation counts reported for aero problems of this size; handling of common-random-number paired evaluations and sub-cell placement noise.
5. **Thin bodies on uniform Cartesian immersed-boundary grids**: how many cells across the thickness are needed for BDIM/IB to give force differences that converge (any numbers for thin plates/airfoils: cells per thickness, cells per chord); the Lauber 2022 BDIM-σ remedy; alternatives (sharp-interface IB, cut-cell) in Julia or GPU codes (Oceananigans immersed boundaries, Trixi, etc.).
6. **Re transfer for ground-effect and multi-element wings**: evidence on whether optimal shapes/topologies found at Re_c 1e3-1e4 persist at 1e5-5e5 (low-Re airfoil optimisation literature: camber/thickness optima vs Re; multi-element slot behaviour vs Re; MAV-scale vs full-scale wing optimisation studies).
7. **GPU options and cost (2026)**: Kaggle GPU quota (T4x2/P100, 30 h/week?), Colab Pro, RTX 5090 (32 GB) street price, A100/H100 spot prices per hour, and whether WaterLily runs on AMD/Apple Metal (Metal.jl) with what performance.
