# Brief A: adjoint toolchains and compute (web research)

Use WebSearch (mode "extended" where results are thin) and WebFetch. Do not write files; return a report (~2000 words, tables preferred, every claim with a citation: DOI/venue/year or URL and access date). Today is 2026-11-01. Where you cannot verify, say "unverified".

Context: a research project wants a generic whole-vehicle (FSAE race car: wings, sidepods, floor, body, suspension arms) aerodynamic shape optimizer. Geometry is a signed distance field (SDF) on a fixed grid; the plan is body-fitted meshes from the SDF (STL → snappyHexMesh or similar) and gradients from an adjoint RANS solver, with hundreds to thousands of regularized node-based design variables. Cloud HPC budget is available. An earlier attempt with OpenFOAM `adjointOptimisationFoam` (frozen turbulence, wall functions, incompressible, 2D ground-effect wing at Re 4.6e5) hit SIGFPE in the adjoint pressure solve; forward-mode AD of an unsteady immersed-boundary solver (WaterLily.jl) had exponentially growing tangents.

Questions:
1. Status (2024-2026) of open-source adjoint RANS toolchains for incompressible external aerodynamics: OpenFOAM `adjointOptimisationFoam` (ESI v2312-v2512: adjoint turbulence models (SA, k-ω SST), wall functions, known stability problems, ATC treatment, documented automotive cases); DAFoam (versions, discrete adjoint via AD of OpenFOAM, SST/SA, wall functions, incompressible solvers, memory/CPU cost per adjoint vs primal, Docker availability, large-case scaling, known issues with separated flow or adjoint linear-solve convergence, automotive examples); SU2 (v8.x discrete adjoint with CoDiPack, incompressible solver, SA/SST adjoint, wall functions, FFD, mesh deformation, cloud scaling, ground-vehicle cases); others worth knowing (Kratos/ShapeModule with Vertex Morphing, commercial STAR-CCM+/Fluent adjoint as references).
2. Robustness of steady RANS adjoint in separated / weakly unsteady flow: evidence and practice (Othmer 2008/2014, Papoutsis-Kiachagias & Giannakoglou, Kavvadias, Helgason, Campobasso & Giles 2004, Krakos & Darmofal 2010, Dwight & Brezillon 2006 frozen-turbulence error), remedies (partial convergence, windowed averaging, residual damping, GMRES recycling, pseudo-transient adjoint, adjoint of a URANS average and its cost, LSS/NILSS applicability), and how industry uses sensitivity maps despite noise.
3. Discrete vs continuous adjoint; accuracy of adjoint gradients vs finite differences on body-fitted meshes with mesh deformation; mesh-sensitivity term; typical agreement levels reported.
4. GPU-accelerated RANS (OpenFOAM with petsc4foam/AmgX, NVIDIA OpenFOAM GPU work 2024-2026, SU2 GPU status) and realistic wall-clock/cost for a 20-50 M cell steady RANS on cloud instances; cloud HPC options and prices (AWS hpc7a/hpc7g, Azure HBv4, GCP H3/C3, Rescale) with $/core-hour and cells-per-core guidance; OpenFOAM strong-scaling guidance.
5. Immersed-boundary / cut-cell RANS at high Re as an alternative to body-fitted meshing: OpenFOAM immersed boundary status, commercial cut-cell (Simcenter, Converge), Cart3D (inviscid), lattice Boltzmann (PowerFLOW; waLBerla/OpenLB) — can any give gradients?
