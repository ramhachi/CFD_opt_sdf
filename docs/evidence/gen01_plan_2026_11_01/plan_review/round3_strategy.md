# W-S review of GEN-01 plan rev 3 (commit 0888d09f; SHA-256 effe84ff... verified): strategy, governance, deliverability

(Opus reviewer, 2026-11-01, verbatim.)

**Verdict: GO_AFTER_FIXES.** Rev 3 goes back to the user's premise: WaterLily, GPU, fixed grid, SDF. But it has four problems. It overstates what a TR-01 pass means. It records goal changes under 決めたこと instead of asking the user. It misstates its roadmap compliance. It asks for a purchase priced about 2x too low, while the cheapest decisive test (does a low-Re element-count preference survive at FSAE Re?) runs last.

## Findings

**W-S1 (P1) §0, §1.3, §6.4, §7. The claim ceiling is incomplete.** §6.4 says PERSISTS or WEAKENED lets the plan write that low-Re topology and shape "keep direction at high Re", but TR-01's evaluator is SA/SST RANS, which this repo has never validated (T2-0b/c/d all NO_GO; EVAL-VALID-01 REFERENCE_INADEQUATE). RE-01 cannot be an "FSAE gate": its own ceiling is Re_c <= 3.8e3. The design space (t >= 15 mm; seed flaps at t/c 8-28 %) excludes real 8-10 mm flaps; even a pass transfers a wing the team would not build. VAL is conditioned on "TR-01 が PASS", which is not a TR-01 label. Outcomes are not mapped to STATE's L1/L2/L3. Fix: a table of outcome -> permitted sentence (MW-01 PASS = an L1/L2 analogue at Re_c 3e3; TR-01 PERSISTS = model-internal RANS retention for thick-element designs, not L3, not physical downforce; RE-01 = no FSAE content); name which TR-01 labels open VAL. Confidence high.

**W-S2 (P1) §0, §3.1, §6.6, §10. Premise changes are recorded as "decided".** (a) The adjoint part of the user's rationale is dropped: rev 3 has no gradient; its topology freedom is the count of instances of one hand-built 7-parameter stadium-plate primitive (MMC type), so "部品ごとの手作りパラメタなしに" is false; spans are fixed (2.5D extrusions); endplates and sidepods need other primitives, which no section defines. Gradient-free search over a primitive library with an integer element count does not itself need an SDF or a fixed grid; what the fixed grid buys is automatic meshing and cheap GPU evaluation, and the plan should say so. (b) "Whole vehicle" is narrowed without a list: floor/diffuser, rear wing, nose, endplate stage and suspension fairings are dropped without being listed. Fix: move (a) and (b) to §10 as user decisions, with a table of dropped components and the reason for each; rewrite §0's promise as "variable element count over a declared primitive library". Confidence high.

**W-S3 (P1) §3.1, §3.4, §6.2. There is no estimand for topology freedom.** The controls (blind hand design, random designs) do not separate "topology freedom helped" from "shape optimisation helped". Knock-out removes an element when its difference is below the floor: that accepts the null, and the power reaches only 2-4 % effects, so the campaign is biased toward fewer elements ("5 -> 1" could be a noise artefact). Six events at alpha = 0.05 each give ~26 % chance of at least one false accept. Fix: a fixed-count arm (same optimiser at the parent's count, equal evaluations, or a 90-evaluation SCREEN-type arm), registered as the topology estimand; knock-out by an equivalence test (TOST with a registered margin) or keep the element by default; handle multiplicity or report events as descriptive. Confidence high.

**W-S4 (P1) §6.0, §7, §3.7. The cheapest decisive test runs last.** Element-count preference is likely Re-dependent: at Re_c 3e3 the boundary layer (delta ~ 5c/sqrt(Re) ~ 27 mm at c = 0.3 m) is wider than the 2.5h = 12.5 mm minimum gap. Yet transfer is decided in interval III, after the purchase and ~2 GPU-weeks. BRIDGE-2D-00 is not "TR-01's 2D version" (single-element pitch/camber/flap slopes on Tyrrell 026; exploratory; trigger-only; second in RE-01's order of things to delay). Fix: an interval I-a before any purchase: (1) TOPO-2D-00: rank 1-, 2- and 3-element 2D families in ground effect at Re_c 3e3 (WaterLily 2D, ~1e5 cells, minutes per evaluation on the M4 Mac or Kaggle) against 2D RANS at 4.6e5 (the T2-0 pipeline on Kaggle CPU, as LOCAL-OF-01 requires), with a concordance rule written in advance, registered or labelled as a user-decision input only; (2) a 2D MW-01 dry run for the optimiser OC and the event rules; (3) ATT-00 before decision (c). Then STAGE-0 (3D, Kaggle), then the hardware decision, then MW-01. Confidence high on the order; medium-high on the physics.

**W-S5 (P1) §3.7, §9. Roadmap compliance is misstated.** R5 reads "no gradient backend on the critical path; no CMA-ES/BO": rev 3 makes CMA-ES and a 30-iteration FD run candidates, so R5 needs amending. #23: GRAD-RPM "replaces FD" if it succeeds, putting a gradient backend on the critical path; the 2026-10-16 entry says "#23 ... no further DIAG rounds". M4/M5 order: M5 (#32, #33, #40) follows M4 (#66), and M4 keeps its dependency on the XV-01 result; running MW-01 in interval II breaks that order unless the user records rev 3 as the M4 decision; §3.7 shrinks M4 to "verifier choice" (M4 is scope, evaluator, or research NO-GO; GPU rental is option C in the 2026-10-24 table). The 2026-10-16 compute addendum (rented-GPU API) and STATE's "no rented API or new spending" are not addressed. #32's Birth-0/1 criteria should become GEOM-S1 acceptance criteria. #80 still describes rev 2. Fix: a §9 writing list naming each amendment, the verbatim user decision behind it, and the issue edits. Confidence high.

**W-S6 (P1) §0, §5, §10(a). The hardware ask is out of date, over-specified, and skips the user's own route.** Price: the Japanese RTX 5090 price passed 1M JPY in September 2026 with none in stock; the plan's 40-60万円 is the 2025 price; the host PC is not costed. 32 GB is not needed: the largest listed case needs 7.7 GB; WaterLily is memory-bandwidth bound (this repo's T4 ~18 ns per cell-step vs the published H100 1.57 ns, roughly the bandwidth ratio), so an RTX 5080 (~240k JPY) or 5070 Ti (~180k) or a used 3090/4090 gives ~50-56 % of the 5090's throughput for 1/4-1/5 of its price. Rental: a 5090 rents for ~$0.35-0.75/h; the programme needs roughly 500-650 5090-hours (STAGE-0, MW-01 with a fixed-count arm, h 2.5 mm checks, GRAD-RPM, SP-01), under ~10万円; this is the route the user named on 2026-10-16; what the user rejected was cloud CPU costing tens of millions of yen. Kaggle: registered runs pin CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 (used_gpu_count 1) on the T4 x2 shape; using both GPUs doubles throughput; a SCREEN-type MW-01 (90 + 126 + 32 evaluations) needs ~110 quota-hours, ~4 weeks; only the 18 h runs at h 2.5 mm exceed the 12 h session limit; "Kaggle cannot run the campaign" is true only for the 720-945-evaluation optimisers, which R5 excludes and which, with k = 8 sigma_d and sigma_d of 1-2 %, need 8-16 % predicted gain per step and will stop NOISE_LIMITED early. Running costs of an owned GPU: ~17k JPY/month at 24/7; connector-melt reports; fit a power limit, UPS, checkpoint/resume; a backend requalification gate is needed (T4 byte references do not carry across GPU architectures; pin the GPU model per registration). Fix: make (a) a priced choice between Kaggle dual-T4, rental, a 16-24 GB card and a 5090; decide after STAGE-0 plus a rented benchmark of the STAGE-0 case; specify one machine (desktop CPU, 128 GB RAM, one GPU). Confidence high on price and memory; medium on rental totals.

**W-S7 (P1) §3.2, §4, §7, §8. PROC-01 coherence.** Stage-0 feeds gates: §7 makes "STAGE-0 の σ_d と費用" a gate, §3.2 designs from Stage-0's sigma_d, §8 lets the STAGE-0 thin-element result change the thickness rule. Restate RE-01's reading: Stage-0 sets engineering parameters only through formulas written in advance; the acceptance sigma_d is measured inside MW-01's first frozen phase on states not reused; the thickness rule is fixed now or derived by a pre-written formula. Inheritance by reference is not acceptable: §4 inherits rev 2 §3.10, a rejected text in git history, whose rules do not map (FAIL includes the Carter weak-condition test but rev 3 has no gradient; the re-registration list includes frozen turbulence; NOISE_LIMITED is defined through pred, which CMA-ES lacks; re-evaluation means a new mesh realisation; INFRA_LOSS means node eviction; budgets are in money and nodes). Restate the protocol for FD trust region, CMA-ES and topology events. §8's "switch to CMA-ES" is a forking path unless in the registered fixed list. Judging the best of 12 per CMA-ES generation needs the SCREEN-01 b_K selection-bias correction. Confidence high.

**W-S8 (P2) §5.3, §5.4. Meshes are still needed.** MW-01's same-Re check needs body-fitted meshes of arbitrary-topology SDF outputs plus long unsteady laminar runs, unpriced (~800 core-h per run; 4 pairs ~4 weeks on the Mac). TR-01 needs a 3D wall-resolved transition-model RANS pipeline; the local high-Re gmsh pipeline is LOCAL_ENV_FAIL, so it must be requalified; 3e6 cells is optimistic. TR-01, RE-01's R1-OF and XV-01 share the Mac. The 20-35 agent-weeks has no breakdown; user hours missing (CAD, blind hand design, procurement, 4-5 registrations with reviews and freezes). Fix: per-stage breakdown anchored on SCREEN-01 and FID-01 history, a mesh-pipeline stage, an unsteady-laminar row in §5.3. Confidence medium-high.

**W-S9 (P2) §6.4. TR-01 rests on one design pair.** Use RE-01's retention slope over several designs (final, fixed-count arm, hand, random-best, event pairs). "32 対" means 32 runs = 16 pairs. Confidence high.

**W-S10 (P2) §3.8, §6.5. The genericity test is underspecified.** The sidepod primitive is undefined; if written after MW-01's code SHA the "no component-specific code" test fails or the held-out geometry leaks. Flux through a solid radiator face is zero; a fixed Darcy-Forchheimer momentum sink would work (fixed, not a design variable). Inlet presence is forced by the flux constraint, so not a free event. "3 accepted steps" is undefined for CMA-ES. Fix: freeze the primitive library at MW-01's SHA; treat SP-01 as a capability test; consider the rear wing as the second topology case. Confidence medium-high.

**W-S11 (P2) §3.6. GRAD-RPM cannot justify replacing FD.** Its gate is a steady case; the main line is an unsteady, likely chaotic wake where tangent sensitivities of long-time averages diverge whatever RPM does (LSS/NILSS address this). Fix: limit the "replaces FD, 1/10 of the evaluations" promise to steady regimes. Confidence medium-high.

**W-S12 (P2) §1.1. The account of why rev 0-2 were rejected.** "数千万円級" is the pessimistic full-scope figure (235 kUSD); rev 2's expected cost was ~77 kUSD (~1,150万円), 25-68 kUSD without CAR-01. "2.7-4.8 TB" is an extrapolation of DAFoam's discrete adjoint; the committed gen_cost_local.py shows a continuous-adjoint line fits in 128 GiB at every stage, with 6-17 months of machine time on 64 cores binding. Rev 3 keeps one noise floor (sigma_d), not "2 つのノイズ床". Round-2 finding R-S1 had already flagged the premise conflict. Fix: state all of this. Confidence high.

**W-S13 (P2) §10. Missing user decisions.** Add W-S2 (a) and (b); the R5 and #23 amendments; whether rev 3 is the M4 decision; the backend choice (W-S6); the RE-01 amendment (promote and extend BRIDGE-2D-00, run ATT-00 before (c)); item (d) "continue as planned" contradicts calling RE-01 "central". Who uses a "low-Re methodology" result: a methodology paper (SMO, Computers & Fluids, JCP) or low-Re/MAV audiences; it gives the car nothing; ask the user whether that outcome alone justifies the spend. Team commitments: wing CAD, the hash of the blind hand design, which rule set applies (JSAE Student Formula Japan vs FSAE 2026). Confidence high.

**W-S14 (P2). Decisions are running ahead of the evidence.** research/ contains no rev 3 reports yet. Hold decision (a) and the MW-01 design until they land.

**W-S15 (P3). Consistency.** The confirmation grid is h 3.3 mm in §3.2 and §6.2 but h 2.5 mm at Re 1e4 in §3.3 and §5.1 (changing Re with the grid confounds R3). "端板" has no stage. STAGE-0 is written up after MW-01 (§6.3).

## What rev 3 gets right
- Restores the premise and the canonical SDF, consistent with STATE's goal line (fixes round-2 R-S1).
- Says plainly that WaterLily cannot reach FSAE Re, that the 3h rule rules out FSAE flaps, and that differences under 2-4 % cannot be judged.
- Exact analytic SDFs remove the need for reinitialisation (#28); boundaries are sharp and there is no Brinkman penalty.
- OpenFOAM is a primal-only verifier, no DAFoam, no cloud CPU.
- Retreat rows written in advance; the second geometry frozen by hash.
- The cost model is in GPU-hours and is a reproducible script.
