# GEN-01 plan rev 1 (7937d12d, SHA-256 a7b74235... verified): round-2 review, angle R-NG (numerical and governance)

(Opus reviewer, 2026-11-01, verbatim.)

**Verdict: GO_AFTER_FIXES.** Rev 1 adopts almost all of round 1 in structure, but five things are still wrong: the registered constant set sits on the boundary of Carter's (14) and leaves out (8)/(15); three pass rules turn noise into FAIL by construction; ADJ-00b's selection still uses exploratory data; G1's in-study sigma rule never reached the text; the budget headline is not the pessimistic bound.

## Findings

**R-NG1 (P1, §3.5). The default set is not admissible, and two of Carter's conditions are missing.** (14) reads xi_g + xi_f1 < 1 - eta_2 (strict), with 0 < eta_1 < eta_2 < 1; below eta_2 the radius must not grow (step (d)). The plan's set gives 0.5 + 0.25 = 0.75 = 1 - 0.25: the boundary, so the set fails (the preprint's §6 summary writes "<=", inconsistent; do not register a boundary set). (8)/(10)/(15) are absent: |ared - cred| <= xi_f2 |cred| with xi_f2 < 1; with eta_1 = 0.1 and pred = 8 sigma an accepted step can have cred = 0.8 sigma, below the 2 sigma error bound (xi_f2 = 2.5), so a "successful" step can make the true objective worse. eta_2 has no operational role. The 2 sigma reading is outside Carter's theorem (§5 names in-expectation enforcement only as an unproved extension); over 30 steps a 2 sigma bound is expected to fail on ~1.4 steps (P(any) = 0.75). Fix: example set (eta_1, eta_2, xi_g, k) = (0.05, 0.1, 0.5, 8) read as a 3 sigma bound (xi_f1 = 0.375; 0.875 < 0.9); add cred >= 3 sigma_step / xi_f2 with xi_f2 = 0.9; write eta_2's radius rule into the algorithm; alternative: keep 2 sigma, eta_2 = 0.1, k = 6 (0.833 < 0.9). Confidence high (preprint pp. 4-5, 19 read).

**R-NG2 (P1, §3.4 item 3, §3.5, §3.10). k = 8 is reachable only in the first few steps of an attached case; no noise-limited stop.** T2-0c morph sigma_eff (cubic fit) on fine30 at h/c 0.224 is 0.00043 Cl = 0.033 % of Cl; as a paired difference 0.047 %; with the n = 16 upper bound (x1.44) 0.067 %. So 8 sigma_step is 0.37-0.54 % of the objective per accepted step, a best case (2D, gmsh, attached, deep convergence). At h/c 0.134 (sigma_eff 0.0021, Cl 1.400) 8 sigma_step is 1.7-2.4 % per step, not achievable (matters for WW-01). 3D snappy-with-layers morph noise is unmeasured. Item 3 (acceptance within 3 radius halvings) needs pred_0 >= 64 sigma_step (~3-4 % per step even in the best case); otherwise once pred < k sigma, item 3 becomes NOT_EVALUABLE = fail. Campaigns will stop on noise long before ||g||_TR <= e_floor / xi_g. Fix: add a NOISE_LIMITED termination (largest pred at Delta_max under the step-displacement bound is below k sigma_step; delivers the last accepted iterate; not a FAIL); evaluate item 3 only over radii with pred >= k sigma_step; register a feasibility formula (expected accepted steps ~ expected gain / (k sigma_step) >= 3, sigma_step from a 3D probe for engineering purposes only); name Sun & Nocedal's noise-relaxed ratio test as the registered alternative. Confidence high on arithmetic, medium on transfer to 3D.

**R-NG3 (P1, §3.4 items 1 and 7, §6.4 "全受理点で成立"). Requiring the weak check at every accepted iterate fails admissible gradients.** With r the true ratio and m the FD response in units of sigma_step, one check falsely fails with probability Phi((0.5 - r) m). Over 30 checks: r 0.8, m 5: 0.87; r 0.8, m 10: 0.04; r 0.7, m 10: 0.50; r 0.6, m 10: 0.99. Near the optimum m shrinks; item 7 caps epsilon so the check becomes NOT_EVALUABLE = FAIL. With active constraints the TR-SQP step is not along g; the check along g_k tests the wrong direction. Fix: make the per-iterate check operational (on a violation, recompute g at greater depth or use Carter's rescaled gradient (53), which the same FD gives for free); gate the campaign on one pre-registered aggregate over checks with m >= m_min (one-sided bound, fixed alpha, OC committed with the ADJ-00b script); send m < m_min to the stopping branch; in constrained stages check along the reduced (tangential) gradient and record the transfer to Heinkenschloss-Vicente as a modelling choice. Confidence high.

**R-NG4 (P1, §6.4, §9). The acceptance constant k is reused as the claim threshold.** k comes from (14) and has no meaning for a claim test. If sigma_claim is the SD of one realisation (T2-0c remeshing 0.2-0.9 % of Cl), the threshold is 8 x 1.44 x sigma_claim = 2.3-10 % of downforce, so WING-3D-01 fails by construction. A paired 16-vs-16 ensemble has a one-sided 95 % margin of about 1.645 sqrt(2/16) sigma_claim = 0.12-0.52 %. §7 uses "3 sigma_claim", contradicting §6.4. Fix: define sigma_claim's unit; replace the claim rule with a registered one-sided test (alpha, the sigma upper bound) on the paired ensemble difference; make §6.4, §7 and §9 consistent. Confidence high.

**R-NG5 (P1, §3.5, §6.0; G1 half-done). G1's in-study sigma rule is not in the text.** No sentence in rev 1 says sigma_step and sigma_claim are measured inside each registered study as a first frozen phase on states not reused, with exploratory sigma setting budgets only; §6.0 still gives LOOP-3D-00 the job "sigma_step の初期値". Fix: add the rule to §3.5; restrict LOOP-3D-00's sigma to budgets through a formula fixed in advance. Confidence high.

**R-NG6 (P1, §4.2-4.3). Confidence high.

**R-NG7 (P1 for the headline, P2 for the omissions; §5, gen_cost.py). Not modelled: ADJ-00 (placeholder), WW-00, EVAL-3D-01; meshing (~170 meshes per stage), storage and idle nodes; the sigma measurement phases on states not reused; item 6's second-base-mesh gradient and item 7's epsilon ladder beyond 2 primals per direction; the other-build and other-code tiers and the second-geometry pass (+2.5-4k at WING-3D-01's high end, so ~10-11k against the 8k cap). Confidence high on arithmetic, medium on the estimates.

**R-NG8 (P2, §3.4 item 2). The OC has no stated hypothesis direction.** With K = 16 the 5 %/95 % quantiles of sqrt F(16,16) are 0.655 and 1.527, confirming the band [0.33, 0.76]. To limit false passes of an inadmissible xi = 0.5 to 5 %, the upper bound must be about 0.33; pass probability is then 0.97, 0.86, 0.63, 0.40 at xi = 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.35; Work F's 0.13 and 0.24 would pass. A bound of 0.75 would pass xi = 0.6 with probability 0.81. "K fixed at 16" conflicts with "the OC decides K". Carter's (13) divides by ||g_k||; the estimator divides by sum FD^2. Fix: put the burden on proving the gradient is good; use sum (adj . d)^2 as the denominator; use the same inner product for the weak and strong checks (Carter (19)-(20)).
