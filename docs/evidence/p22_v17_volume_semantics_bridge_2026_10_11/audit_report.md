## Why the historical 0.12925 numbers agree

`8272 = 8×1034` and `(0.025)³ = (0.05)³/8`, so the v17 hard count equals the
historical handoff 1034-cell discrete volume. The source STL's independently
recomputed signed triangle volume is `0.1292499984670429 m³`, differing from
the v17 hard value by `1.5329571223521299e-9 m³` (relative `1.1860403408383902e-8`).
The loaded mesh is watertight and consistently wound. This is recorded in
`source_surface_volume_diagnostic.json`, using existing `trimesh` rather than
a new volume algorithm. These are distinct derivations with close numerical
values, not evidence that #51 substituted the handoff measure or that a
continuum convergence study passed. No exact cell-by-cell STL partition
identity, trilinear zero-set equivalence, or general bias correction is inferred.
