# GRIDRES-01 prerun note (flow_40 kernel)

Date: 2026-10-20. Plan: `docs/gridres01_plan_2026_10_20.md` with Amendment 1 (G1-G6) and Amendment 2 (H1-H4; they override the text above them). Evidence class (target): bounded multi-grid actual-primal response measurement at one parent along one direction, Re 80, Kaggle T4.

**The first freeze is superseded.** Commit `a4a52f3f` (freeze at source `9d6aa8e0`, kernel `cfd-opt-sdf-gridres01-f40`) was never submitted: the pre-run review found the multigrid-depth confound and required Amendment 2. Its freeze, inventory, identity record and rendered kernel are removed from the tree by the commit that re-freezes (history is untouched); nothing here edits or reinterprets them.

**Question.** At the OPT-01 v1 parent along the frozen Stage B direction, flow_24 and flow_32 disagree on the downforce response (flow_24 negative curvature, no capability; flow_32 near-linear, +4.75e-4 N at 1.25 mm). Does flow_40 side with flow_32, with flow_24, or neither?

**Run order (H2).** One kernel, seven runs, each at most 1800 s: (1) the instrumented OPT-01 parent on flow_24, (2) on flow_32, then (3-7) the five flow_40 states parent, +0.3125, +0.625, +1.25 and -1.25 mm along the Stage A robust direction. The reruns come first so that an instrumentation that is not passive is detected before the long runs: the runner compares each rerun's force CSV with the retained Stage B parent CSV (hash in the inventory, derived from the pinned Stage B analysis) and stops the kernel on any difference; the analyzer refuses such an output as an integrity failure (no analysis is written). Aggregate solver cap 10800 s, kernel timeout 21600 s. Kernel `ramhachi888/cfd-opt-sdf-gridres01-f40`. One submission, no automatic resubmission or repair.

**States (G2).** parent, +0.3125, +0.625, +1.25 and -1.25 mm: byte-identical to `docs/evidence/opt01b_stage_b_2026_10_15/inventory.json` (every phi hash re-derived from the pinned parent and direction raw files by `build_gridres01_inputs.py`; the state names are the Stage B names). The flow_24/flow_32 values entering the fits are the retained Stage B CSVs, hash-checked against the Stage B analysis (the reruns only provide Poisson statistics and the passivity check); the flow_40 parent is a plain fresh run, no cross-grid byte identity exists.

**Grid construction (G1).** `scripts/waterlily_gridres01_job.jl` builds the case with the frozen `V16W4Sensitivity.build_case(id, n, BASE_MAX_M, origin, 0.025)` and validates it locally (n in 24, 32, 40, 48: dims 150x72x54, 200x96x72, 250x120x90, 300x144x108, spacing 0.8/n m, nu = n/80, Re 80, origin, box). No file under `julia/CFDSDFWaterLily/src` is edited; solver settings, time window [80, 120] tU/L, sampling and the Candidate C operator are those of the Stage B jobs (the flow_32 job differs from the flow_24 job only in the case id; the gridres job differs from the flow_32 job only in the case construction/validation and the probe).

**Passive Poisson probe (H1).** `scripts/gridres01_poisson_probe.jl`, called after the force evaluation at every sample, reads only `sim.pois.n` (the host vector of iteration counts that WaterLily's own `solver!` pushes, two solves per step: sliced since the previous sample) and `L2(sim.pois.levels[1])` (r.r of the finest-level residual left by the last solve, the quantity of the solver's own tol test; a read-only reduction). Nothing writes a field or changes a setting. Per run it writes `<case>.poisson.csv`. CPU proof of passivity: `scripts/gridres01_poisson_passivity_check.jl` runs a small 3-D WaterLily simulation (2 control runs without the probe, then one with it) and requires byte-identical force text and u/p fields (`tests/test_gridres01_job.py`). This proves the probe code is passive in WaterLily 1.8.0 on CPU; it does not prove GPU reproducibility between Kaggle sessions, which is what the rerun byte-identity gate tests.

**Poisson reporting.** WaterLily 1.8.0 `solver!(ml; tol=1e-4, itmx=32)` (`MultiLevelPoisson.jl:108`); the multigrid has 4 (flow_24), 6 (flow_32), 4 (flow_40) and 5 (flow_48) levels (counted on CPU in the pre-run review). Per run the analyzer reports the fraction of solves that used all 32 iterations, iteration quantiles over all solves and quantiles of the per-sample final residual. Label `POISSON_CONFOUNDED` (descriptive, never gating, never changes the verdict) iff the cap-hit fraction of flow_24, or of the worst of the five flow_40 states, exceeds that of the flow_32 parent by more than 0.10 absolute; a missing label does not exclude a pressure-solver effect.

**Caps (G2, Amendment 2).** Per-run timeout 1800 s, aggregate solver cap 10800 s, kernel timeout 21600 s (must equal `kaggle kernels push --timeout`).

**Quantities and verdict (G3, G4; downforce).** Per grid the changes relative to the parent (time-weighted window means, as Stage B) are fitted without intercept to a*s + b*s^2 on the four matched steps (the parent is a zero row); also the forward-only fit and the max residual. G_max = max(0, sup over s in (0, 1.25] mm of a*s + b*s^2). `FLOW40_SIDES_WITH_FLOW32` iff G_max(flow_40) > 4e-5 N; `FLOW40_SIDES_WITH_FLOW24` iff G_max < 2e-5 N (both strict; the edges are AMBIGUOUS); `AMBIGUOUS` otherwise (then the flow_48 branch below); `INCOMPLETE` on a downforce fit residual above 2e-5 N on any of flow_24, flow_32, flow_40. Report-only: contraction ratios for a and b (CONTRACTING if both <= 0.6), THIRD_VALUE, acceptance emulation per tested step (gain > 3e-5 N and drag change <= 0), drag a and b per grid. Retained matched-subset fits (computed by the analyzer from the real CSVs): flow_24 a = 9.8e-5, b = -2.28e-4 (residual 4.8e-6 N, G_max 1.05e-5 N); flow_32 a = 4.73e-4, b = -7.68e-5 (residual 9.9e-6 N, G_max 4.71e-4 N); the plan's all-six-state figures agree within a few percent.

**Reading notes (H4).**
- The verdict uses the fit that includes the -1.25 mm reverse state (a report-only probe for the gates). The forward-only G_max is reported without classification.
- With 3 degrees of freedom per fit a single outlier is partly absorbed: read G_max together with the acceptance emulation per tested step, not alone.
- A residual-gate INCOMPLETE is a scientific outcome: the analysis is written with all deltas, fits, the labelled verdict and a `verdict_reason`. Only integrity failures (including a non-byte-identical parent rerun) refuse to write.
- Submission command (exactly; `kaggle` 2.2.4): `kaggle kernels push -p infra/kaggle/kernel_gridres01_f40 --accelerator NvidiaTeslaT4 --timeout 21600`. Re-run the identity-free check (`freeze_gridres01.py --check-identities`, read-only) before the push of any re-freeze; the committed record is valid for 24 h.
- flow_48 must be split into two kernels (Kaggle GPU session limit) before any flow_48 freeze; nothing about flow_48 is frozen or run here. The conditional branch is as in the plan: only if the flow_40 verdict is AMBIGUOUS, same rule applied to flow_48 and reported jointly, with its own identity check, freeze, review and joint-reading rule.

**Use of the outcome (G5).** The direction was selected as the max-min of the flow_24/flow_32 predictions: a selected line, not a random draw. No GRID-01 amendment can rest on this result alone. SIDES_WITH_FLOW32 justifies the next step of broadening (the four Stage A basis directions at +-1.25 mm on flow_40, about 2.4 h) before any amendment; SIDES_WITH_FLOW24 keeps the both-grid rule and points at flow_32; AMBIGUOUS leads to flow_48 as above.

**Disclosures (G6, H1).** Non-blind: the flow_24/flow_32 values and the quadratic structure are known before the run. Lattice commensurability confound: the design lattice (0.025 m) equals the flow_32 spacing, flow_24 is coarser, flow_40/48 finer; the sequence changes resolution and lattice alignment together, and any convergence is that of this interpolated-lattice body only. Multigrid-depth confound: the grids differ in multigrid depth (4, 6, 4, 5 levels) under a 32-iteration cap, so part of the flow_24/flow_32 disagreement may be pressure-solver convergence rather than resolution, and flow_40 shares flow_24's shallow hierarchy; the Poisson statistics describe this and do not remove it. The 3e-5 N floor is nominal; the 1e-5 N guard band is the scale of the fit residuals, not a noise measurement. Not claimed: grid independence, physical or Stage V downforce, a gradient, optimizer behaviour, flag qualification.
