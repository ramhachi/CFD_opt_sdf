# MODE-01 (#60) solver-free prescreen of smooth modes of the OPT-01 v1 parent

**Registration status: `EXPLORATORY_COMMITTED` (PROC-01).** Claim ceiling: geometry and lattice-representability facts only. No solver ran, nothing here feeds a flag, threshold, gate, `shape_update_allowed` or any downforce claim; the mode basis used by the registered M2 (#61) is frozen in M2's own registration. All qualification flags stay false.

**Visible before this run:** the roadmap diagnosis (R2: wide-support smooth modes), the OPT-01 v1 parent bytes and the 13 GEOM-02 profile-v2 gates (the parent passes all of them, N = 8272 = cap exactly), the GRID-02/OPT-01 slope results. Not visible before: how these modes fare against the gates (the outcome below was not predicted; the expectation was that rigid transforms would mostly pass).

## What was built
- `scripts/mode01_modes.py`: mode generators (`phi_new = trilinear resample of the parent`, outside the design mask the parent bytes are kept, no reinitialisation, float64 arithmetic, float32 result, deterministic). Sign conventions are in `UNITS` of that file: pitch + = nose down (low-x end toward the ground) about the solid-node centroid; heave + = up; surge + = downstream (negative control); camber `a` = tip deflection, mean z kept; thickness `phi - a`; aspect = x by (1+e), z by 1/(1+e); bumps = Gaussian (sigma 0.125 m medium, 0.05 m small, added by me) normal offset on the ground-facing / upward-facing surface, cosine taper 0.05 m in |phi|; D1/P1 = the grad03 directions, `phi + a d`.
- `scripts/mode01_prescreen.py`: 77 states on the registered parent (family `opt01`) and 41 on a diagnostic re-genesis parent (family `regen6p25`). Gates are the repo's own: `evaluate_opt01_v1_states.state_record` (13 GEOM-02 v2 gates incl. topology/component events, mask preservation, clearance, volume_semantics; P22-v2 count; retained LOWDIM volume band 10 % and clearance 0.15 m). Extra columns: support fraction, eikonal median/p95, smoothed volume change, phi SHA-256 (Fortran order) of every state, a cheap numpy replica of the crossing-slope count (`crossing_slope_fail_nodes`, equals the gate's `nodes_below_threshold`), and the proposed P22 projection (largest `delta <= 0`, `phi - delta` on active nodes, bisection, re-gated in full; not registered).
- Outputs: `prescreen.json`, `prescreen_table.md` (opt01); `prescreen_regen6p25.json`, `prescreen_table_regen6p25.md`. Every state's recomputed phi hash matched the gated one.
- `tests/test_mode01.py` (18 pass, 1 slow test with `MODE01_SLOW=1`).

## Result 1: the registered parent admits no wide-support mode at large amplitude
Of 76 (mode, amplitude) states, 13 pass every hard gate, none of them a wide rigid/analytic mode: `thickness -1 mm`, `bump_lower/bump_upper -2,-5,-10 mm`, `bump_small -2,-5,-10 mm`, `P1_upstream_lobe -1,-2,-5 mm`. All pitch, heave, surge, camber, aspect states fail (gate failures over 77 states: `near_zero_gradient_band` 58, `min_feature_width` 38, `sdf_distance_property` 10, `export_surface_integrity` 1, `volume_semantics` 2). Component count stays 1 everywhere, clearance (margin >= 0.32 m), mask preservation, component connectivity and topology classification always pass, the 10 % smoothed-volume band (vs the v17 baseline) fails only for thickness +-3, +-5 and -2 mm, N_phi is at or below 8272 except `thickness +5` (8328) and `aspect +0.04` (8410) (projection deltas -1.66 mm and -1.15 mm, both still failing other gates).

**Sign asymmetry:** only the *inward* sign passes (`bump -a` passes, `bump +a` fails `near_zero_gradient_band` with 31-90 crossing-slope nodes). A +a/-a paired difference cannot have both ends gate-clean on this parent.

## Result 2: cause (measured, mechanism partly inferred)
1. **Tie plateau.** 4,327 of 107,415 active nodes have |phi| < 1e-3 m: the parent's faces lie on lattice node planes (phi ~ 0 with random sign at the mm level, exact zero counted as fluid). The crossing-slope rule needs `|dphi|/h >= 0.5` between opposite-sign neighbours; any perturbation that is not uniformly positive flips plateau nodes at random and creates near-tie pairs (|phi| <= 4 mm, slope 1e-4..0.17). Sub-cell heave 5 mm: 809 failing nodes (parent 0), 691 of them in the interior at the stair-step layers; `heave 25 mm`/`surge 25 mm` (whole spacing, snapped coordinates) have 0 failing nodes.
2. **Pinned end-plane layers.** The parent's y end faces sit exactly on the node layers y = +-0.45 m, one layer outside the design mask (y <= 0.425), with phi ~ 1e-8 (652 nodes). Those layers must stay byte-identical, so any x-z move of the interior mismatches them at the rim. This alone fails `min_feature_width` for a whole-spacing shift (`surge 25`: min solid width 0.025 < 0.0375; with all nodes shifted 0.05). The modes cannot "not move y": they do not, but the end faces stay pinned at the old x-z outline.
3. **Thin parent.** The slab is 2-3 cells thick (parent min solid width 0.05 against the 0.0375 limit), so any sub-cell redistribution of occupancy creates 1-cell spots (0.025).
4. **Interpolation smoothing:** `sdf_distance_property` (median |grad|-1 deviation <= 0.1) fails for pitch >= 2 deg and for heave/surge 10 mm (about half a cell, maximal interpolation smoothing; 20 and 30 mm pass this gate).
Ad-hoc experiments (not in the committed outputs, reproducible with the committed functions): a cosine ramp of the amplitude to zero over y in [0.3, 0.425] fixed most `min_feature_width` failures from the pinned layers but not the crossing-slope failures (still 350-670); uniform global offsets of phi (applied to every node): offsets of about 6 mm or more in either direction removed the crossing-slope failures of heave/surge 5 mm, an offset of a whole cell (25 mm) recreated them, 3 mm did not help.

## Result 3: diagnostic re-genesis (family `regen6p25`, a proposal, not registered)
`phi - 6.25 mm` on every node (a quarter cell: off the node planes and off the cell centres) plus a design mask widened by the two end-plane layers the offset turns solid; its own N (8328) is the P22 cap and its own smoothed volume the band base. A first attempt with a 12.5 mm offset was discarded: it put solid outside the registered design mask (the zero-amplitude parent failed mask preservation) and half-cell offsets put the zero level on cell centres, so N_phi became very unstable (heave 10 mm: +16 %).
Strict passes (5): `thickness -2`, `bump_lower -5`, `bump_upper -5`, `bump_small -5`, `P1_upstream_lobe -2`. **Fail only on the P22 count** (every GEOM-02 gate other than volume_semantics passes, volume band and clearance pass): `heave +-30 mm` (N 8657/8623, +4 %), `surge +-30 mm` (N 8603/8653, +3.3 %), `thickness +2`, `bump_small +5`. So a relative count rule (e.g. N within a few percent of the parent) instead of the hard cap would make heave 30 and surge 30 (the negative control) fully gate-clean; pitch, camber, aspect still fail `near_zero_gradient_band`/`min_feature_width` (pitch 0.5 deg: 10-36 nodes). The P22 projection cannot rescue them: 32 of 41 states need it (delta -0.5 to -5.2 mm), the projected states re-fail the plateau gates (heave/surge 30 after delta -3.4 mm fail again), and where the projected state passes the delta *is* the mode (`thickness +2`: delta -1.92 mm, 96 % of the mode's peak; `thickness +5`: 98 %; bumps 25-41 %). The projection should therefore not be used with wide positive modes.

## Support (fraction of active interface-band nodes, |parent phi| <= 2h, with |dphi| > 10 % of the band maximum)
thickness 1.00 > heave 0.76-0.84 > pitch 0.76-0.80 > aspect 0.66-0.69 > camber 0.57 > surge 0.37-0.47 (translation changes phi only where the normal has an x component) > D1 0.19, P1 0.23-0.25 > bump_lower/upper 0.05 > bump_small 0.008. **Deviation:** the registered medium bump (sigma 0.125 m, one surface) has a *smaller* node fraction than the grad03 small references D1/P1, because those are rough fields spread over the whole band. The ladder wide > medium > small by node fraction only holds with my added `bump_small` (sigma 0.05 m); D1/P1 are small in smoothness, not in support. UNC-01 should state which of the two axes it tests.

## Recommended amplitudes for UNC-01 (design inputs, not a registration)
Unknown without a solver which amplitude gives "several percent downforce"; amplitudes below are chosen for geometric largeness within what the gates tolerate (max band displacement and smoothed volume change are in the tables). Gate-failing states are usable only as declared exploratory uncertainty probes, never as candidates.

## Problems and deviations
- The expected outcome (wide rigid modes pass) is false on this parent; I did not register any change. Options for the roadmap's "offset re-genesis" decision: (a) quarter-cell offset plus widened design mask plus a relative P22 rule (gives heave/surge only), (b) additionally thicken the slab (a 18.75 mm offset gives parent min width 0.1 and N +57 %, which is not a sensible P22), (c) accept only inward-sign differences on the registered parent.
- Volume neutrality is analytic for pitch/heave/surge/aspect, but the smoothed volume changes by -1 to -7 % through interpolation of the thin slab (tables); thickness +-1 mm already moves it by 4.5 %.
- Surge is not support-wide (0.37); it is a control by physics, not by the support metric.
- The registered-parent batch was started before the cheap `crossing_slope_fail_nodes` column and the `n_max`/`volume_vs_gate_base` fields existed; those fields were added to the cached rows by recomputation from the (hash-verified) states, and the final json was assembled from the cache. The committed code reproduces the same rows.
- A 12.5 mm re-genesis run was discarded (invalid construction, see above); not committed.
