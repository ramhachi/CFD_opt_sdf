# SCREEN-01 (#64) registration — decision memo, revision 2 (2026-10-31)

Companion to `docs/screen01_registration_2026_10_30.md` (revision 2, `DRAFT_REVIEWED_REV2`). Revision 0 of this memo (commit `c0a17ea2`) recorded the first decisions; the plan review (`plan_review/{numerical,governance,strategy}.md`, decisions in `plan_review/adoption.md`) changed several of them (revision 1); the source review (`source_review/{rules,pool_oc_check,build_harness}.md`, decisions in `source_review/adoption.md`) changed the binding layer, the loss protocol, the caps and the OC disclosures (revision 2, D8, D9, D11, D12). Each decision below gives the alternatives, the choice, the reason and the residual risk, with the review findings that drove a change. Nothing here is a new measurement; no force of any SCREEN state exists.

## D1. Objective and constraint (unchanged in substance; decision rule changed)
**Alternatives.** (a) paired flow_32 downforce gain `I = Delta_bar_DF / J_b,48` subject to drag **non-increase** (allowance 0); (b) a drag budget; (c) an efficiency objective; (d) downforce alone.
**Choice: (a).** Precedent `DRAG_ALLOWANCE_N = 0.0` (LOWDIM-03/04, OPT-01b), the preparation note, and the form XV-01 re-evaluates. (b) has no registered value and, at any value admitting pitch, the answer is "pitch +3.1" (M2: +15.4 % downforce, +10.8 % drag, already measured on both solvers) — a trivial L1; the strategy review's surrogate confirms it (L/D alone → the corner (1, -1, -1), +24.9 % downforce, +14.8 % drag, 39 % of the box passing; budgets +1 %/+2 % → unsourced values). (c) is inconsistent with the registered g_min mapping. Under (a) the visible data make the problem a genuine trade (`Delta_DF / Delta_drag`: reflex 1.25, pitch 1.4-1.5, C1 2.3, twist 2.9, camber -19.7 6.5): camber buys downforce cheaply, pitch - or reflex + sells it at about 1.4 to recover drag.
**Rules (rev. 1).** The response surface targets `Delta_drag_pred <= -m_D`; the measured feasibility filter and the confirmation decision use the point sign `Delta_bar_drag <= 0` with four resolution labels, both violation forms blocking GAIN. **The gain rule is the FID tier form** `Delta_bar_DF,s >= g_min J_b,48 and > 3 SE_eff` (review G7: the rev. 0 one-sided lower bound was a raised target by 3-5 % of x*, contrary to "no additional floor"; it is now the label `GAIN_RESOLVED_ABOVE_MIN`). **`GAIN` may carry an unresolved drag constraint** (review G9; rev. 0's memo wording "only resolved favourable rules give GAIN" was wrong for D): `PASS_UNRESOLVED` is the expected label at the FID drag sigma_d, and XV-01 re-evaluates drag on OpenFOAM with an SE about 1e-4 N.
**Why the point sign.** A resolved non-increase at P = 4 would demand a measured drag decrease of about 8e-4 N; an optimizer puts its finalist on the boundary, where that rule rejects it most of the time. The end-to-end OC (registration §5.10) shows the registered combination gives a high P(GAIN) when the surrogate's trade exists, and never passes the null.
**Drag estimator (exact).** Same-kernel same-phase paired differences; `sigma_d` with P - 1 dof; `SE_eff = max(sigma_d, 1.364e-4 N) / sqrt(P)`. flow_48 drag is a sign label only.
**Residual risk.** A true small drag increase (about +3e-4 N) passes the confirmation point rule about 20 % of the time (caught at XV). The drag neutrality of a camber-plus-trim finalist relies on OpenFOAM retaining camber's drag the way it retains pitch's and reflex's; camber's drag retention is untested in FID (`XV_DRAG_MARGIN` disclosure, review S8).

## D2. Search space (changed: subset fixed, camber box extended)
**Alternatives.** Five modes with a measured ranking step (rev. 0); the three strongest modes fixed a priori; heave free; camber beyond A*; a narrower pitch box.
**Choice.** **Pitch, camber, reflex fixed before any force** (review S6/N11: the visible single-mode ranking is pitch 4.1e-2 > reflex 1.5e-2 > camber 1.3e-2 >> twist 1.6e-3 >> sweep 1e-4 N; a ranking step would have a predictable result, the nine unused subset pools would cost 30-40 CPU h, and twist/sweep are null levers at their gate-clean amplitudes). Heave = 0 (unchanged: FID tier B, vehicle setting, the rigid part of the z systematic; review G12: this does not remove the z systematic of the shape modes, which is now in the claim ceiling). **Camber box u in [-1.5, 1]** (review N1b/S5): the surrogate's constrained optimum sits on the camber -1 face at about 1.4-1.6 g_min, which the lower-bound/point rules and model error can erode; the extension raises the ceiling to about 2.2-2.4 g_min (surrogate). Cost: no FID fidelity evidence beyond 19.7 mm camber (FID retention decreases with camber amplitude: 0.77 at 11.3, 0.61 at 19.7), a lower admissible fraction (29.3 and 41.1 failed R0 at the native placement), and a fit over a wider range; every point is gated, the design pool reaches into the extension (lowest design camber u = -1.29; the refinement optimum sits on the u = -1.5 face, so the proposals are a mild extrapolation of the fitted quadratic by about 0.2 u in camber, source review Q5), XV measures the finalist, and the handoff flags a finalist in the extension. The OC's camber-saturation variants (D11) quantify the case the fidelity caveat describes. Pitch stays |u| <= 1 (the ±3.1 axial points anchor the fit's curvature; the refinement is local anyway); the narrower pitch box was not adopted.
**Residual risk.** A finalist at camber beyond -19.7 is a fidelity extrapolation for XV. The stage-1 screen of the roadmap is reduced to the six axial points (§14).

## D3. Finite admissible pool (changed: admissibility, extension rules, pool size)
**Choice.** Scrambled Sobol in the rev. 1 box: the design pool (seed 20261030) 64 points, +64 while fewer than 12 admissible, at most 256; the random-control sequence (seed 20261033) consumed to 19 admissible or 512 points; the hand-design ladder and the downforce reference. **Admissible = gate stack + X1 + STL chain check** (review G6: the former post-force G5a check now runs before any force). The extension rules are implemented (`screen01_pool.py p0`); the terminal rule is a pre-freeze stop (an inadmissible design at 256 → the registration is not frozen; never a force-driven refill). The ten-subset P0 of rev. 0 is dropped (one subset, about 120 members, 2-3 h wall on 4 workers).
**Why Sobol and not the ladder lattice.** Products of ladder points alias with the lattice phases and give R0 a structured pass pattern; scrambled Sobol spreads the R0 lottery evenly and is reproducible by seed. R0 is kept because GEOM-03 returned NO_GO for the R3 composite.
**Evidence (geometry only).** Rev. 0 P0 on the unextended box: 27 of 64 admissible (42 %, every failure R0), design rank 10 (9 no-intercept) / cond 7.5 (4.2), all 41 gate-clean states X1-clean, 3 of 16 5-D random points admissible. Rev. 1 P0 on the extended box: 35 of 64 design-pool members admissible (7 of 13 in the extension), design rank 9 / cond 3.51 (no-intercept), M = `v18__camber_m19.7_pitch_m0.38`, random control 19 admissible in 48 points (details `pool_prescreen_rev1/README.md`, registration §3.4).
**Residual risk.** Admissibility at the native placement only (as FID). The admissible fraction in the camber extension may be low; the refinement then proposes nearer -19.7 (no harm beyond a lower ceiling).

## D4. Response surface, refinement and finalist (changed: refinement step added, hull removed)
**Alternatives.** (a) rev. 0: proposals = the best in-hull unevaluated pool members by prediction; (b) a continuous argmax built as a new geometry (one point); (c) a gated refinement cloud around the constrained argmax (reviews N1a/S1); (d) a 5-D surface.
**Choice: (c)**, `screen01_pool.propose`: the no-intercept quadratic (9 coefficients) fitted on the 6 axial + 9 D-optimal points; for each target `tau in {1, 1.5, 2}` the constrained argmax over the 81^3 box grid with `ẑ <= -tau m_D`; a 256-point scrambled-Sobol cloud of radius 0.25 u around it, filtered to predicted-feasible points within 10 % of the predicted optimum, ordered by predicted downforce (the argmax itself first; a target with no predicted gain contributes nothing); the three lists interleaved into one queue (a point shared by two targets gated once, owned by the first list) and gated (full admissibility) in rounds of 4 until each target with a queued point has 2 admissible points of its own or 36 points were gated; proposals = the best admissible per target, then by prediction, at most 4 (registration §5.2, §5.11). The finalist is the measured-feasible candidate with the largest exploration downforce among the 6 + 9 + <= 4 states.
**Reasons.** Both quantitative reviews showed that (a) cannot work: the drag-feasible region with downforce >= g_min is about 0.2 % of the box (a thin wedge on the camber face), none of the 27 admissible rev. 0 pool members lay in it, the hull covered 20-30 % of the wedge, and P(GAIN) was 0.05-2.5 % with 96-99 % of runs ending in an uninterpretable `GAIN_BELOW_MIN_AT_SELECTION`. (b) meets the R0 lottery once (about 50 % rejection) and offers no hedge against drag model error. (c) gates up to 36 points so admissibility costs proposals, not the search direction, and the staggered targets hedge the drag model error (the argmax lies on the predicted boundary, where half the proposals would be truly infeasible). The box replaces the hull (review S2) because the optimum sits on a box face the D-optimal hull barely touched. Proposals are measured, so model error affects what is proposed, not the validity of the decision; the `POOL_LIMITED` label (review S3) says when the surface predicted a feasible gain that no evaluated candidate reached.
**OC comparison (registration §5.10).** on the visible-data surrogate the revision-0 procedure gives P(GAIN) 2.8 % (misfit-heavy 11.4 %), the revision-1 procedure 91.2 % (frozen design 88.7 %, unextended box 82.5 %, m_D 1.0e-3 86.6 %, single target 88.2 %, noise x1.5 83.7 %, misfit-heavy 72.9 %, admissible fraction 0.25 89.2 %, response scaled 0.8 / 0.6 / 0.42 86.3 / 71.3 / 34.4 %, **camber response saturated at u = -1 / -1.25: 70.8 / 88.0 %**, null 0.0 %; the 2026-10-31 rerun after the source review (p_adm 0.54, four variants added), the two earlier rev. 1 runs gave 90.2 and 89.7 % for the registered row with every common entry within 3 points); the reviewers' independent rev. 0 estimates were 0.05 % and 2.5 %, their refinement estimates 43-71 %. The headline rests on the camber response continuing beyond -19.7 mm (source review Q2): if it saturates there, the procedure is a 0.71 procedure with 18 % `GAIN_BELOW_MIN_AT_SELECTION`; the registration says so (§5.10), and no rule depends on it.
**Residual risk.** The refinement adds about 40 min of solver-free gating between G1 and G2 and a round trip; the fit is a quadratic over a box with visible cubic behaviour in pitch (review N10), handled by measuring the proposals and by the misfit-heavy OC variant.

## D5. Phases and grids (unchanged; disclosures added)

## D6. Selection bias (changed: sigma_disc re-sourced)
**Choice.** Decision estimator = the confirmation estimate at g_min exactly (no raised target); disclosure with the **primary `sigma_disc = 5.61e-4 N`** (recomputed: FID tier-A WaterLily-to-V1 residual SD about the through-origin slope 0.655; the retention SD 0.058 x 1e-2 N agrees), the OpenFOAM-internal G3-set residual SD 1.4474e-3 N only as an upper bound (reviews N4/G13: rev. 0 mis-sourced it), `E[max_K]` for the actual K (1.844 at K = 19), central (0.655) and pessimistic (0.57) expected OpenFOAM gains and margin labels, and the statement that the attenuation and the effective K make `b_K` conservative. At K = 19 the central `XV_MARGIN` boundary is `I_s = 2.32 %` and the pessimistic one `3.23 %`.
**Reasons.** #64 and the handoff forbid floors on top of g_min; the within-WaterLily bias is removed by fresh phases; the WaterLily-to-OpenFOAM bias belongs to XV, which is the holdout; the label gives XV the number it needs. The rev. 0 pre-commitment of XV was replaced by "eligible for registration" (review G14).

## D7. Controls (changed: feasible hand design, matched random control, chain controls)
**Choice.** M = camber -A* with a pitch trim sized from the visible linear ratios so the predicted drag is -m_D (fallback ladder: reflex trim, 0.75x, 1.25x, half/half; first admissible entry), run at the exploration and confirmation phases (review G4: rev. 0's C1 has +5.8e-3 N drag and is infeasible by construction; C1 stays as a downforce reference for a chain check of a known design difference). R = the first 19 admissible points of a separate Sobol sequence in the same 3-mode box, K_rand = K_opt = 19, measured in G1 (review G5: rev. 0's 5-D random points were near the parent and diluted by null modes); `|u|_inf` distributions recorded, `AMPLITUDE_MISMATCH` and `K_MISMATCH` labels; R-best gets the same confirmation. NC at the exploration and confirmation phases, a within-kernel parent repeat in g1k11 and f32a (determinism → INVALID), a phase-11 parent replay in f32a (drift label) (review G8). **Rejected:** running R and M only after a finalist exists (review S7; a conditional R needs its own exploration kernel and a second confirmation generation; with the redesign the no-finalist branch is a minority and the control comparison is informative in NO_GAIN too); a null candidate (identical to the repeat); a z-shift NC (UNC-type work).
**Compared on:** confirmation per-phase differences finalist minus control with resolution labels; `RANDOM_FEASIBLE_GAIN`; the exploration versions.

## D8. Budget and stopping (changed: counts, rule)
Seven formal kernels (g1k11 4.6 h, g1k12 4.5 h, g2 <= 1.2 h, f48k13/14 2.0 h each, f32a 1.5 h, f32b 1.2 h) = 17.0 GPU h + four smokes (about 1.45 h; the G2 proposals need their own campaign `screen01_g2` because the harness binds each campaign SHA into its run identities) = about 18.4 GPU h; cap 24 GPU h of formal session wall with `T` counting every unfinished kernel (reviews N7/G19), a budget record written once before every push after G1 (bound by freeze 2.1 / 3, later ones under `wl/`; source review B7); flow_48 pushed first in F; exceeding the cap stops the pushes and goes to the user (INCOMPLETE (BUDGET) if not resumed, the un-pushed kernels declared `--not-pushed KERNEL=BUDGET` with the stop record; source review R5); one G1 rerun fits, two do not. Equal budget: 38 exploration runs per side. **Caps (rev. 2, source review B4):** the rev. 1 aggregate caps were 1.19x (g1) and 1.22x (f48) the slowest measured run, so a session 20 % slower than the slowest seen would have voided a 39-run kernel at its tail; the rule is now 1.5x the registered maximum run count x the per-run estimate, rounded up to 100 s, timeout = cap + 3,300 s (g1k11 24,300 / 27,600 s; every timeout <= 7.7 h). Alternatives: the reviewer's 1.6x (no reason to prefer it; 1.5x already exceeds every measured session-to-session spread, 7.5-16 %, by a factor of three), or raising only g1 and f48 (rejected: one stated margin for every kernel is simpler to audit). Cost of the higher caps: a pathological session wastes up to the cap before it trips (bounded by the 12 h session and counted in `T`).

## D9. Outcome classes (changed: data loss, search_complete, R3 split)
`GAIN` (G PASS, D point sign <= 0 with any resolution label, R3 CONFIRMED); `NO_GAIN` (no feasible candidate or below minimum at selection with the search complete; G FAIL; drag violation resolved or unresolved; R3 point sign split); `INCONCLUSIVE` (G or R3 unresolved); `INCOMPLETE` (data loss that stops the search or the confirmation, budget; reviews G1-G3: rev. 0 let infrastructure loss become NO_GAIN); `INVALID` (NC, nondeterminism). Non-finite, cap-exceeded, verify-failed or implausible (> 25 % of the parent force) state-phases are DATA_LOSS (review G3). Nothing is repaired from a force.
**Kernel loss (rev. 2, source review R3/B3).** Alternatives: (a) rev. 1: a verify failure or a signature match is `INFRA_LOSS` (one rerun), everything else (a divergent, capped or crashed state) is never rerun; (b) one identical rerun for every kernel without a verified table, the cause classified as a record; (c) a finer mechanical classification of the runner's ERROR into "the state's fault" (no rerun) and "the session's fault" (rerun). **Choice: (b).** Under harness v1.1 any failed state voids its kernel (the runner stops, verify is all-or-nothing), so (a) made the registered state-phase loss a kernel loss and left an un-rerun kernel undeclarable (`--lost` accepted only the five signature ids); (c) needs failure messages of WaterLily/Julia that have never been observed and would still misclassify a transient CUDA error as the state's fault. A byte-identical rerun cannot select on a force (same bytes, same phase, same pinned runner; the first verified output is authoritative), so (b) is a spending rule inside the §4.3 cap, not an acceptance rule; the signature table keeps its purpose as the mechanical record of the cause (`WALL_CAP`, `STATE_FAILED`, `NONFINITE` added). Rejected mitigations of the kernel-level unit of loss: a G1 kernel split (about 25 GPU min, more sessions, a §4.2 restructuring after the review; no divergent state in about 150 harness runs of this plate) and a SCREEN-side continuing runner (a harness change). Residual risk: a deterministic divergence costs one wasted rerun (4.6 h for a G1 kernel) before the kernel is declared lost; a second such kernel would trip the budget rule and go to the user.
**Written-once outputs on every branch (rev. 2, source review R1, R12).** Freeze 3 is written after every `finalist` (with the campaigns only when F is pushed) so that `main` binds on the no-finalist branch too; `rs.json` and `finalist.json` report the G1 chain controls (`g1_controls`, cross-checked), and a failing one stops the pushes mechanically (the builder refuses to generate the later campaigns) because the outcome is `SCREEN01_INVALID` at step 2 whatever G2 or F would show. Alternative rejected: pushing F anyway and letting `main` say INVALID (about 11 GPU h for descriptive data on an invalid chain).

## D12. P0 re-validation and the cache key (rev. 2, source review Q1, B5, Q7)
**Alternatives.** (a) reuse the 2026-10-30 cached rows under a re-key justified by the git evidence that no gate-stack file changed between P0 and the review (true at `48388bff`: `git diff --stat` empty for the reused code and `src/`); (b) re-run P0 from empty caches under a key that covers what the admissibility decision depends on. **Choice: (b).** The rev. 1 key (`fid01_designs.DEPENDENCIES`) covered neither `fid01_designs.py` itself, nor `fid01_build.py`, nor the gate-stack core under `src/cfd_sdf/design/`, nor the four data files the stack reads (policy JSON, v17 volume baseline, OPT-01 parent, genesis record); the X1 records and the STLs carried no key at all. "Re-validated under the reviewed fingerprint" must mean recomputation under a key that would notice a change; 2.6 h of unattended CPU is cheap against 36 registered states' admissibility. `screen01_pool.DEPENDENCIES` (65 files, the observed import closure plus the data files, asserted by `selfcheck`) is the key for gate rows, X1 records and the parent record, STLs are read only when they hash to their record, error rows are never cached and refuse freeze 1. The re-run must reproduce every flag, SHA and volume of the 2026-10-30 record (a difference is a defect that stops the registration). Residual risk: none of substance; the cost is the 2.6 h and the regeneration of `states.json` and the raws (they embed the P0 file hashes).
