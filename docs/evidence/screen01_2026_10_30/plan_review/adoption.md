## Reviewer disagreements resolved
- Governance saw no redesign need; numerical and strategy required one. The redesign is minimal (G1/G2/F/A kept, rules kept) and the governance fixes are all adopted, so both verdicts are satisfied.
- Strategy proposed a 96-128 point cloud gated in full (about 2 h); numerical proposed 8-12 points. Chosen: a 256-point cloud filtered by prediction, gated in order with a cap of 36 points (about 40 min on 4 workers), which gives >= 2 admissible points per target with probability above 0.99 at the measured admissible fraction.
- The lower-bound G rule (rev. 0) against #64's "no additional floor" (G7) and the numerical reviewer's acceptance of it: the point rule is adopted because g_min is to be applied exactly as registered; the lower-bound status is reported.
