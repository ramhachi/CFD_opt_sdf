## 2. What does not change, and what changes in meaning
Unchanged code and numbers: lattice (origin (-1, -0.8, -0.6) m, h = 0.025 m, 121 x 65 x 49), design/fixed/forbidden/root masks (the v17 baseline masks = the OPT-01 v1 parent masks), topology/reinitialisation policy ids, narrow band h, flow domain, moving ground at z = -0.9 m, flow grids and jobs, Re 80, reference length 0.8 m and area 0.64 m^2, force definitions (drag = +Fx, downforce = -Fz, Candidate C body), P22-v2 code and cap (`N_phi <= 8272`), the GEOM-02 v2 profile `geom02_v17_research_gates_v2_crossing_slope_2026_10_15` with policy d4cd9aa4…, the retained gates (smoothed-volume band 10 % vs the v17 baseline, clearance margin >= 0.15 m).
Changed in meaning (stated, not hidden):
- **P22-v2 is applied unchanged to a second parent** on the same lattice; it is not re-registered. Its registration describes a constraint *active at zero slack* on the v17 baseline; on v18 (N_phi 7544, slack 728 = 8.8 %) it is a non-binding guard by design. The 10 % smoothed-volume band carries the volume constraint. The M2 freeze repeats this statement; a #55 comment records it.
- **The volume band is centred on v18 by construction**: t is chosen so the v18 smoothed volume equals the v17 baseline smoothed volume. Smoothed volume is a representation-dependent measure (one-sided cosine step, about V - (h/2) A); v18 is -8.8 % in sharp count and its continuum volume (0.1205 m^3) is 6.8 % below the v17 sharp volume (0.12925 m^3).
- The min_feature_width gate margin of the parent is 0.098 m against 0.0375 m (v17 parent 0.05): the gate is far from binding on the parent but is the binding gate under sub-cell motion (§3.3).

## 3. v18 parent

### 3.1 Shape (rev. 2)
A stadium cross-section (thickness t, semicircular leading and trailing edges of radius t/2) extruded over |y| <= y_tip with **flat tips**; exact SDF of the extrusion:
`d2 = sqrt(max(|u| - L/2, 0)^2 + w^2) - t/2`, `dy = |y| - y_tip`, `phi = min(max(d2, dy), 0) + sqrt(max(d2, 0)^2 + max(dy, 0)^2)`, with `u = (x - xc) cos(alpha) + (z - zc) sin(alpha)`, `w = -(x - xc) sin(alpha) + (z - zc) cos(alpha)`. Float64 at every node, stored `<f4`.

### 3.2 Parameter rules
- Bounding box = the v17 baseline solid-cell box x in [-0.6, 0.55], z in [-0.25, 0.25] m: `xc = -0.025`, `zc = 0`, `L cos(alpha) + t = 1.15`, `L sin(alpha) + t = 0.5` (keeps leading/trailing edge x, top and the **ride height**: lowest zero-level z = -0.25 m, 0.65 m above the ground). alpha and L are computed from t, not rounded.
- `y_tip = 0.45 - h/2 = 0.4375` m.
- t: bisection on [0.10, 0.13] m, 40 halvings, of `smoothed_volume_m3` (`build_lowdim01_inputs.geometry`, contract `sdf_native_smoothed_volume_v1` primitive) against the v17 baseline value, then rounded to 0.1 mm: t = 0.1153 m (4.6 h), alpha = 20.395 deg, L = 1.1039 m, chord 1.2192 m (exact values in `genesis.json`).
- Masks/policies from the v17 baseline; `generation = 0`; `source_sha256` = SHA-256 of the sorted-key JSON of the parameter dict.

### 3.3 Why (S0)
- Inclined faces never lie on node planes (the v17 tie plateau, failure 1, cannot occur there).
- A flat face parallel to a node plane, at offset delta from the outer layer j = 14 (outside the design mask), satisfies the crossing-slope rule on both sides of the end layer only at delta = h/2 (rim nodes of j = 15 have slope (delta + |phi|)/h toward j = 14 and (eps + h - delta)/h inside the layer). So the tips sit on the cell-centre plane: those end cells have corner mean exactly 0 (fluid), deterministically and independently of every x-z mode (their eight corners are dy-dominated); the hard-count invariance radius is therefore 0 and is reported, not used.
- Round (Minkowski) tips were tried in rev. 1: the crossing-slope rule fails near normals ~(1,1,1)/sqrt(3) for curvature radius below about 4.3 h (corners have t/2 = 2.4 h), and the rounded tip sections create one-cell slivers. Committed probe (`tip_probe.json`, 40 seeded rigid placements): round tips 1/40 crossing, 33/40 width failures; flat tips 0/40 crossing, 16/40 width failures.
- The remaining width failures are one-cell nubs of the cell occupancy at the extremal column of the rounded leading/trailing edges (radius 2.4 h): the GEOM-02 width estimate (2·EDT ridge on 4x-repeated cell occupancy) is not continuous in sub-cell placement for a smooth body of this thickness. This is a property of the gate on smooth bodies; it is not relaxed here (§6).

## 4. Declared modes (S2)
All modes are evaluated analytically (no resampling, no reinitialisation) on the active nodes; every other node keeps the parent bytes. The **gated bytes** are the harness state model's reconstruction `construct_state(parent, d / max|d|, 1000 max|d| mm, +1)` with `d` the analytic change (`v18_plate.harness_state`); they differ from the analytic values by at most a few float32 ulp. eta = clip(y / y_tip, -1, 1), s = clip(2u/L, -1, 1). Ladders are incommensurate with h = 25 mm and with each other (no two points of a rigid mode share a sub-cell phase).

| Mode | Definition | Sign (+) | Volume | Ladder (each +-) | Class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| heave | zc + a | up | exact (rigid) | 7.3, 13.7, 21.3, 31.1 mm | wide |
| pitch | alpha + a about (xc, zc) | nose down (higher incidence; lowers the leading edge by about 9.6 mm/deg) | exact (rigid) | 0.7, 1.3, 2.1, 3.1 deg | wide |
| camber | w -> w + a (s^2 - 1/3) | mid-chord up, edges down (a = mid-minus-edge deflection) | exact (shear) | 11.3, 19.7, 29.3, 41.1 mm | wide |
| reflex | w -> w + a (s^3 - 3s/5) | trailing edge down, leading edge up | exact (shear) | 11.3, 19.7, 29.3, 41.1 mm | wide |
| thick_chord | t (1 + e), L re-solved for equal section area L t + pi t^2 / 4; centre, alpha, y_tip kept | thicker and shorter (a chord-length/thickness trade; the edges move about 57 mm at e = 0.1) | exact | e = 0.025, 0.05, 0.1 | wide |
| twist | alpha(y) = alpha + a (eta^2 - 1/3) about (xc, zc) | tips nose down relative to mid-span | exact (sectional rigid) | 1.1, 2.3, 3.1, 4.7 deg | wide |
| dihedral | zc(y) = zc + a (eta^2 - 1/3) | tips up | exact (shear) | 11.3, 19.7, 29.3, 43.1 mm | wide |
| sweep | xc(y) = xc + a (eta^2 - 1/3) | tips downstream | exact (shear) | 11.3, 19.7, 29.3, 43.1 mm | wide |
| surge | xc + a | downstream | exact (rigid) | 7.3, 13.7, 21.3, 31.1 mm | control (by physics) |
| twist_anti | alpha(y) = alpha + a eta | +y tip nose down | exact | 1.1, 2.3, 3.1 deg | control (mirror-odd: exact-physics D(+a) = D(-a)) |
| thickness_D0 | phi - a | thicker | not neutral | 1, 2, 3 mm | reference (XFID D0 anchor) |
| offset_s400 / s125 / s050 | phi - a g taper, g = exp(-((x - xc)^2 + y^2) / (2 sigma^2)), sigma 0.4 / 0.125 / 0.05 m, both surfaces, taper of `mode01_modes` | outward | not neutral | 2, 5 mm | support-large / -medium / -small |
| corrugation | phi - a cos(2 pi u / 0.1 m) cos(2 pi y / 0.1 m) taper | outward at crests | about neutral | 2, 5 mm | rough (wide extent, low smoothness) |
The parent is point-symmetric about the node (xc, zc) and mirror-symmetric in y, so the +A and -A states of heave, surge, camber, dihedral, sweep and twist_anti are lattice-congruent (mirror images): their pair is one gate sample. The shear/sectional modes are not exact distance fields; their eikonal error is second order on the faces and first order near the tips, measured by the gates. Eikonal p95/max include the stale nodes outside the design mask next to the tips (reported, not gated).

## 5. Gates and the basis rule (applied once)
- Hard gates per state, identical to `scripts/mode01_prescreen.evaluate` (`evaluate_opt01_v1_states.state_record`): the 13 GEOM-02 v2 gates with the v18 parent as `parent_state` (the parent row uses itself), P22-v2 `N_phi <= 8272`, smoothed-volume band 10 % vs the v17 baseline, clearance margin >= 0.15 m. A gate reported `unmeasured` blocks like a fail.
- Reported, not gated: support fraction (MODE-01 definition), max interface change, volume change vs the parent (smoothed, sharp), eikonal median/p95, crossing-slope failing nodes, lowest zero-level z, harness step (mm), max |gated - analytic|, the parent's hard-count invariance (`build_lowdim04a_inputs.invariance`).
- **Wide member**: both +A and -A gate-clean at some ladder A, with max interface change >= 5 mm (h/5, the XFID D0 amplitude at which WaterLily and OpenFOAM agreed within 2 %) and support fraction >= 0.5 at both signs; A* = the largest such A. A wide mode whose |cos| (symmetric phi-difference at A*, active band) with an earlier member in table order exceeds 0.9 is not counted as a distinct direction; the singular values of the member set are reported.
- **Control member**: both signs gate-clean at some A (no support requirement). **Support probe / reference / rough**: at least one gate-clean state (either sign) at some A.
- **Outcome**: `V18_RUN_INVALID` if any state errors or a row is missing or the parent bytes differ from `genesis.json`; `V18_PARENT_FAIL` if the parent fails a gate; `V18_BASIS_READY` if at least 6 distinct wide members, at least one control member, and gate-clean support-medium and support-small probes exist (basis = distinct wide members + controls, at most 10); otherwise `V18_BASIS_INCOMPLETE`. Consequences: READY → M2 draft proceeds to its freeze TODOs; INCOMPLETE → recorded fail-closed with the failing gates per mode, #60 stays open, the next step (e.g. a width-gate decision for smooth bodies, or another parent) is a new plan and a new index row; PARENT_FAIL/RUN_INVALID → diagnose, no re-run under this plan without a dated amendment. No gate, ladder or shape rule changes after the run.
- The prescreen does not choose the M2 amplitudes; M2 may use only tested ladder points.

## 6. Risks and limits
- The min_feature_width estimate fails at about 40 % of random sub-cell placements of this plate (S0). The prescreen selects gate-clean states; an optimiser over v18 (M3b) would meet the same rejection rate unless the width gate is re-registered for smooth bodies, which is a separate decision not taken here.
- Lattice-congruent ± pairs halve the gate information for six modes.
- The modes leave the j = 14/50 layers next to the tips at parent values (>= 12.5 mm, fluid); no solid outside the mask.
