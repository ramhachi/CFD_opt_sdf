## Question and why it comes before re-genesis or a larger basis

OPT-01 v1 Stage B (`docs/evidence/opt01b_stage_b_2026_10_15/opt01b_analysis.json`, `OPT01_V1_REJECT`) ran the same seven geometries on flow_24 and flow_32. A solver-free quadratic refit
of the retained numbers (planner and two reviewers, descriptive) shows the grids agree on the sign of slope and curvature of the downforce response along the Stage B direction and
disagree in magnitude: dL(s) = b s + a s^2 with s in mm gives flow_24 b = +9.4e-5 N/mm, a = -2.2e-4 N/mm^2 (zero crossing ~0.42 mm, peak at s* = b/(2|a|) ~ 0.21 mm with value
b^2/(4|a|) ~ 1.0e-5 N, below the 3e-5 N floor) and flow_32 b = +4.9e-4, a = -0.9e-4 to -1.0e-4 (zero crossing ~5.4 mm; s* ~ 2.7 mm lies beyond the tested 1.25 mm, so the extrapolated peak
~ 6.7e-4 N is an extrapolation; the fitted value at 1.25 mm is ~ 4.7e-4 N); residuals are 5e-6 / 8e-6 N (intercept-free fits on the four forward points). The binding constraint is therefore a grid
dependence of slope (about 4.8x) and curvature (about 3x), not opposite responses. Which grid's slope is closer to a resolved one, and whether the difference comes from
resolution or from how the flow grid is placed relative to the design lattice (below), decides every later accept/reject rule; re-genesis or a larger basis would not.

## Known confound: flow-grid placement vs the design lattice (h = 0.025 m)

Forces are sampled at cell centres (WaterLily `pressure_force`/`viscous_force`); BDIM coefficients at face points. The flow origin (-2.5, -1.2, -0.9) is an integer multiple of h from the SDF origin
(-1.0, -0.8, -0.6) on all axes, so a cell centre lies on a design node plane (per axis) iff 16(2k+1) is divisible by n: flow_16 every cell, flow_24 and flow_48 every third cell, flow_32 never
(cell centres at the exact design-cell centre, uniform phase 1/2), n = 64 never. The retained response experiments all use the pair (24, 32), so none can separate resolution from this
placement. Control: rigidly translate the body relative to the flow grid by (h/2, h/2, 0) through the existing pinned job value `W4_CANONICAL_ORIGIN_M` = (-0.9875, -0.7875, -0.6) with
the SAME phi bytes (z is not shifted: ride height unchanged; z-plane alignment is therefore only partly inverted and the control is partial, disclosed). Slopes/curvatures are compared
within each placement; the parent downforce itself changes with placement and is reported as a placement effect.

## Registered quantities

Per kernel/state: host-recomputed downforce and drag (N). dL_X(s) and dD_X(s) are relative to the SAME KERNEL's parent. Resolution of grid X: max(3e-5 N, the largest parent-pair
difference observed on X); the floor is a nominal flow_24 value, unmeasured on other grids, and a byte-identical pair measures reproducibility, not noise.
For each cell X in {16n, 24n, 24s, 32n, 32s, 48n} (n = native placement, s = shifted): least-squares fit dL(s) = b s + a s^2 to the forward points (flow_48: 4 forward points; 7 or 8 states
elsewhere use forward points for the primary fit and the reverse points as a check), reporting b, a, the intercept check c of dL = c + b s + a s^2, max residual, and, for a < 0 and b > 0 (b <= 0 is checked first: "no forward gain"; a >= 0 with b > 0: "unbounded in
range"), the zero crossing b/|a|, the peak step s* = b/(2|a|), the attainable gain b^2/(4|a|) at the peak, whether s* is within the tested range (s* <= 1.25 mm; beyond it the peak is an
extrapolation) and the in-range maximum (value at min(s*, 1.25 mm)); and the sign status of every dL (`positive_resolved` > +res, `negative_resolved` < -res, `unresolved`). Also the 4-point (n = 16, 24, 32, 48)
tables of b, a and of the parent downforce/drag against n, the drag changes, and the OPT-01 both-grid rule evaluated descriptively on (24n, 32n) (known REJECT), (24s, 32s), (32n, 48n) and
48n alone. No pair is selected on this result and this result alone does not amend the dual-grid rule.
Descriptive robustness fields (never change the verdict): per cell the model-free central slope b_c = (dL(+1.25 mm) - dL(-1.25 mm))/2.5, b with intercept, the leave-one-out b values (each forward
point dropped once), and, per classification, whether the alignment class, the fine class and the STABLE flag would change if b_c (resp. b with intercept) replaced b ('unchanged', the other
value, or 'not computable'); b_48n with all four forward points referenced to the f48a parent (instead of the f48b repeat parent for +1.25 mm) and the same sensitivity; for the alignment
control also gap_after_shift_ratio = 1 - m24 - m32 and |b_24s - b_32s|.

## Pre-registered classification and decision map (thresholds fixed now, not tuned)

Alignment control (uses b; a is secondary and always reported). Gap G = b_32n - b_24n; m24 = (b_24s - b_24n)/G (change of the flow_24 slope under the shift, as a fraction of G, positive = in
the direction of the native flow_32 slope); m32 = (b_32n - b_32s)/G (the same for flow_32, positive = in the direction of the native flow_24 slope). No lattice-phase mapping is assumed: the shifted
flow_24 sits at phases 1/2, 5/6, 1/6 in x and y, and the shifted flow_32 becomes node-aligned in x and y, so "toward each other" is a statement about the slope values only. The shifted flow_24 and
flow_32 parents must differ from the native parents (downforce or drag), else the study is `GRID02_INCOMPLETE` (shift ineffective: the job ignored the origin).
- `GRID02_ALIGNMENT_DOMINANT`: m24 >= 0.5 and m32 >= 0.5.
- `GRID02_ALIGNMENT_NEGLIGIBLE`: |m24| <= 0.2 and |m32| <= 0.2.
- `GRID02_ALIGNMENT_PARTIAL_OR_MIXED`: anything else.
Fine-grid position (flow_48n): r = (b_48n - b_24n)/G; `FINE_CONTINUES_TO_FLOW32` r >= 0.5, `FINE_REVERSES_TOWARD_FLOW24` r <= 0.2, `FINE_INTERMEDIATE` otherwise; flag `STABLE_32_TO_48` iff |b_48n - b_32n| <= 0.3 |b_32n|.
Integrity failure: `GRID02_INCOMPLETE` (no `--write`).
Decision map (recorded next step; none is executed by this registration):
The map branches only on the alignment class and the STABLE flag; the not-stable branch merges "still growing" and "turning back".
- ALIGNMENT_DOMINANT: the slopes are sensitive to the (h/2, h/2, 0) shift; next = register a placement-robust evaluation (native + shifted average) before any new accept rule; no basis expansion.
- ALIGNMENT_NEGLIGIBLE and STABLE_32_TO_48: flow_32 slope is within 30% of flow_48 for this response; next = proposals on flow_32 with a flow_48 finalist
  confirmation, and a separately registered amendment of the dual-grid rule (cost per candidate: (24, 32) ~ 2 + 6 min, (32, 48) ~ 6 + 33 min, i.e. ~ 6x; a finalist-only flow_48 check ~ 1.1x).
- ALIGNMENT_NEGLIGIBLE and not STABLE_32_TO_48: no affordable grid gives a stable sub-5 mm slope; next = larger-step / coarser-design-lattice study (solver-free preparation first).
- ALIGNMENT_PARTIAL_OR_MIXED: a second placement phase study (other x/y phases, and z at a registered ride-height tolerance) before any rule change.

## Known before the run (disclosure)

OPT-01 v1 Stage B, GRID-01, the W4 history and the retained quadratic refit above are visible. Hindcast, not a precise prediction: with baseline downforce 0.2545 / 0.3316 / 0.3613 N at
n = 16 / 24 / 32 and 1/n or 1/n^2 scaling of the changes, the flow_48 parent downforce is expected near 0.38-0.40 N. No prediction is registered for b_48 or a_48. FD-07 (a lattice-phase shift of
(0.27, 0.37, 0.43) h changed the flow_24 short-window baseline by +11.9% drag, +7.2% downforce, a re-sampling not a pure translation) is weak prior support that placement matters. The 3e-5 N
floor is unmeasured on flow_16/32/48. G = b_32n - b_24n = 3.95e-4 N/mm is already known from the retained OPT-01 Stage B data (b_24n = 9.4e-5, b_32n = 4.9e-4, intercept-free fits); only
b_24s, b_32s and b_48n are new. The absolute thresholds are |db| >= 1.98e-4 N/mm (0.5 G) and |db| <= 7.9e-5 N/mm (0.2 G); with these retained b values STABLE_32_TO_48 implies r in
[0.63, 1.38], i.e. FINE_CONTINUES_TO_FLOW32.

direction). All qualification flags false, `shape_update_allowed=false`, delta unset, FD-08/GRAD-03 verdicts unchanged.

## Changes from v1

Primary estimands changed from a sign classification at two steps to (b, a, zero crossing, attainable gain) with named thresholds; the vacuous solver-free covariate (it was ~0 on the actual parent and
step-independent) was replaced by a rigid-translation control run in the same study; the infeasible n = 64 follow-up was dropped; flow_48 reduced to the 7 states that identify b and a plus one reverse
and a repeat; resolution and parent reference pinned per kernel; the cost model, aggregate cap and the flow_16 alignment arithmetic were corrected; claims and the decision map were rewritten; "opposite
response" was corrected to "same-sign slope and curvature, different magnitude".

headroom on the first runs; descriptive robustness fields, the gap-after-shift ratio and the shift-effective integrity gate added; decision-map wording made non-causal; the known-before-run
disclosure extended (G and the STABLE implication are already known); freeze closure and checks tightened. No threshold or classification rule changed.
