## 1. Question, hypothesis and claim ceiling
**Question (L1, roadmap M3b-1; #64).** Does a gradient-free, fixed-topology procedure — a quadratic response surface on the three strongest analytic modes of the v18 plate, a solver-free gated refinement of the surface's constrained optimum inside the registered geometry gates, and a finalist chosen by a fixed rule from the measured candidates — find, under drag non-increase, a WaterLily flow_32 paired downforce gain of at least `g_min` on fresh placement phases, with the same resolved sign on flow_48?

Claim ceiling: Re = 80 reduced-laminar WaterLily (Kaggle T4), one analytic parent (v18), three analytic wide modes (pitch, camber, reflex) with heave fixed at 0 and twist/sweep excluded, the box of §3.1 (camber extended to -29.55 mm, an amplitude with no FID fidelity evidence), in-plane placement phases only (every candidate carries an unphased z-lattice systematic that no phase averages and that the fresh-phase estimate does not remove; heave = 0 removes only its rigid part), the registered R0 width gate with its placement artefact, exploration and confirmation phases that are different deterministic points of the R2 torus (not independent draws). A `SCREEN01_GAIN` is a statement about **one finalist's paired same-phase differences against the parent inside the WaterLily solver family**; `g_min` is judged on flow_32 only, flow_48 contributes the resolved sign and labels. It is not independent verification (XV-01 #65 is), not an optimized-shape claim beyond L1, not physical downforce, not grid independence, not FSAE, not high Re. The finalist's expected OpenFOAM gain is a planning projection (§5.7), not a result.

Outcomes (§5.8): `SCREEN01_GAIN`, `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN` (with a cause), `SCREEN01_INCONCLUSIVE` (search complete, a rule unresolved by noise), `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (data loss or budget; includes a search that could not be completed), `SCREEN01_INVALID` (a chain control fails: NC or determinism). Registered secondary labels (no effect on the outcome): drag resolution, `GAIN_RESOLVED_ABOVE_MIN`, `R3_RATIO_IN_BAND`, `G48_ABOVE_MIN`, `R3_E2_EXCEEDED`, `R3_DRAG_SAME_SIGN`, `POOL_LIMITED`, `RS_FIT_{OK,POOR}`, `OPT_VS_RANDOM`, `OPT_VS_MANUAL`, `RANDOM_FEASIBLE_GAIN`, `AMPLITUDE_MISMATCH`, `K_MISMATCH`, `XV_MARGIN_{ADEQUATE,THIN}` (central and pessimistic), `XV_DRAG_MARGIN`, `GENERATION_DRIFT`.

## 3. Search space, pool and controls
### 3.1 Modes, box and normalisation
The subset is **fixed before any force: pitch, camber, reflex** (`v18_plate.UNITS` signs: pitch + nose down, camber + mid-chord up, reflex + trailing edge down). The choice uses visible values (§8): on flow_32 the single-mode downforce differences at A* are pitch 4.1e-2, reflex 1.5e-2, camber 1.3e-2, twist 1.6e-3, sweep about 1e-4 N (an 8x gap between the third and the fourth mode), so a measured ranking step would have a predictable result and the roadmap's "one mode at a time" screen is reduced to the six axial points of this subset (§14). **Heave is fixed at 0** (a SCREEN decision: pure heave failed FID tier B, the ride height is a vehicle setting, and a heave component would add the rigid part of the z-lattice systematic). Twist and sweep are excluded (null levers at their gate-clean amplitudes). Local/narrow modes stay excluded.

Box in normalised coordinates `a_m = u_m A*_m` with the MODE-01 v18 A* (pitch 3.1 deg, camber 19.7 mm, reflex 29.3 mm): `u_pitch in [-1, 1]`, **`u_camber in [-1.5, 1]`** (camber down to -29.55 mm; the MODE-01 ladder points 29.3 and 41.1 failed R0 at the native placement, so the extension is a region of lower admissible fraction, every point gated individually), `u_reflex in [-1, 1]`. Nothing outside the box is built. Interior and joint points are not MODE-01-tested; the FID fidelity evidence covers camber amplitudes 11.3 and 19.7 mm only, so a finalist in the extension rests on XV-01 for its fidelity (disclosed in the handoff).

### 3.2 Construction
A candidate is a dict `{mode: amplitude}` built by `scripts/fid01/fid01_designs.py` on the v18 parent: the modes are applied **jointly** to the plate parameters and `v18_plate.plate_phi` is evaluated once (an exact plate SDF); only active nodes change; the evaluated bytes are the harness state model `construct_state(parent, d / max|d|, 1000 max|d| mm, +1)`. State names are `fid01_designs.name(spec)` with the spec keys in sorted order; amplitudes are rounded to 1e-3 mm or deg.

**Pools** (`screen01_pool.py gen`; scrambled Sobol `scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol(d = 3, scramble = True, seed)`, `u = lo + (hi - lo) x`):

### 3.6 Width rule and the disclosed selection bias of admissibility
Admissibility uses the registered R0 at the native placement, unchanged (GEOM-03 did not qualify R3). R0 fails about 40-60 % of sub-cell placements of this plate for a one-cell occupancy artefact (P0: 58 % of the joint 3-mode points in the unextended box, 45 % in the rev. 1 box, 52 % of the random-control points). Consequences, stated: (i) the design, the refinement proposals and the finalist are admissible members, not the best points of the box; (ii) the refinement gates up to 36 points around the predicted optimum so that admissibility costs proposals, not the search direction; (iii) admissibility is placement-dependent and is not re-evaluated at the exploration or confirmation phases; (iv) no failure is repaired, re-sampled or re-phased. The same filter applies to R and M.

## 5. Rules (computed by the frozen `scripts/screen01/analyze_screen01.py`, cross-checked by `screen01_check.py`)
Responses: downforce (objective) and drag (constraint), N, as the harness state-force tables report them. Differences are candidate minus parent at the same phase in the same kernel.

### 5.1 Estimators and data loss
- For a state s and a phase set Φ (the phases common to s and the parent in the stage's kernels, after data loss): `Delta_bar = mean_k(F_s,k - F_parent,k)`, `sigma_d` (|Φ| - 1 dof) as M2 (`analyze_unc01.stats`); `SE_eff = max(sigma_d, floor) / sqrt(|Φ|)` with the M2 floors `0.00020904201605451784 N` (downforce) and `0.000136408347135453 N` (drag); with |Φ| = 1, `SE_eff = floor`. `t = t_{0.95, |Φ|-1}` where a t is used (labels only). Gains are reported as `I = Delta_bar / J_b,48`.
- **DATA_LOSS (state-phase):** a run whose force is non-finite, whose job exceeded its per-state cap or did not finish, whose harness verify failed, or whose `|F - F_parent,k| > 0.25 |F_parent,k|` for either response (frozen implausibility bound; the largest registered difference is 15 %) is excluded from every estimator and counted in §5.9. A lost kernel loses every state-phase in it.
- Exploration estimates use Φ ⊆ {11, 12}; confirmation estimates use Φ ⊆ {13, 14, 15, 16} (flow_32) and {13, 14} (flow_48). They are never pooled.

### 5.2 Response surface and the refinement step (`rs`, written once after G1)
On the design points with data (6 axial + 9 design; a point is dropped only if both its phases are lost) fit by ordinary least squares, for downforce `y` and drag `z`, the no-intercept full quadratic `f(u) = b'u + u'Qu` (9 coefficients; `f(0) = 0` exactly because a difference vanishes at the parent). Report coefficients, residual SD, the lack-of-fit ratio against the pooled per-point `SE_eff` (`RS_FIT_POOR` if the residual SD exceeds 3x the pooled SE_eff; descriptive), and the matrix rank and condition number; if rank < 9 or cond > 20 → `RS_NOT_FIT` (no proposals; `search_complete = false` if a loss caused it). Report the constrained optimum over the box grid (81 points per axis), `u*_1`, `y*_1 = max ŷ s.t. ẑ <= -m_D`, and `POOL_LIMITED_PRE` = (`y*_1 >= g_min J_b,48`).

**Refinement (`screen01_pool.propose`, deterministic; parameters frozen):** `m_D = 1.5e-3 N`; targets `tau in {1, 1.5, 2}`; for each target the constrained argmax `u*_tau` over the 81^3 box grid with `ẑ <= -tau m_D`; the candidate list = `u*_tau` followed by a scrambled-Sobol cloud of 256 points (seed `20261032 + target index`) of max-norm radius 0.25 u around `u*_tau`, clipped to the box, kept if `ẑ <= -tau m_D` and `ŷ >= 0.9 ŷ(u*_tau)`, ordered by `ŷ` descending, excluding already-evaluated names; the three lists interleaved round-robin; gated (full admissibility, §3.3) in rounds of 4 points in that order, stopping after the round in which every target with candidates has >= 2 admissible points or 36 points were gated. **Proposals**: per target the admissible point with the largest `ŷ`, then the remaining admissible points by `ŷ`, at most 4. A target whose constraint has no grid point, or no admissible point, contributes nothing (recorded). The whole gating record (every point, its predictions and gate results) is written in `rs.json`; proposals are new states (direction raws + hashes written into the G2 campaign `screen01_g2` by the frozen builder, freeze 2.1). No continuous argmax is run as a geometry, no extrapolation outside the box, nothing is chosen by a force.

### 5.3 G2
The proposals are run at phases 11 and 12 with the parent. No further proposals, no second round.

### 5.4 Finalist, R-best (`finalist`, written once after G2)
Candidate set `C_opt` = the 6 axial states, the 9 design points and the proposals (`K_opt = |C_opt| <= 19`). **Feasible** = `Delta_bar_drag <= 0` (exploration point estimate; the registered precedent `DRAG_ALLOWANCE_N = 0.0`). **Finalist** = the feasible candidate with the largest exploration `Delta_bar_DF`; ties (exact equality) → registered order (axial in mode order + before -, design points in freeze order, proposals in rank order). **Futility**: if `C_opt` has no feasible member, or the finalist's `Delta_bar_DF < g_min J_b,48 - 3 SE_eff` (the FID tier convention for resolution; it cannot reach §5.5 G), there is no finalist and F is not pushed; the outcome is `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN` (cause `NO_FEASIBLE_CANDIDATE` / `GAIN_BELOW_MIN_AT_SELECTION`) if `search_complete`, else `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (§5.9). **`search_complete`** = every registered G1 state has both exploration phases, `rs` was fit, every proposal slot is either filled or empty for lack of admissible candidates (not for loss), and every proposal has both phases. **`POOL_LIMITED`** = `POOL_LIMITED_PRE` and no feasible candidate's exploration `Delta_bar_DF >= g_min J_b,48` (the surface predicted a feasible gain the evaluated candidates did not reach; descriptive, carried to the result). **R-best** = the same rule over the 19 random states; none feasible → R-best empty (`RANDOM_NO_FEASIBLE`). Output: both selections, `K_opt`, `K_rand`, every candidate's exploration estimates, the confirmation run lists.

### 5.5 Confirmation decision (`main`, written once after F)
For the finalist f (and, descriptively, R-best and M) with the confirmation flow_32 phases Φ_s (|Φ_s| >= 3 required for f, else `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`):
- **G (gain, the FID tier form; g_min exactly as registered):** `PASS` if `Delta_bar_DF,s >= g_min J_b,48` and `Delta_bar_DF,s > 3 SE_eff,DF`; `UNRESOLVED` if `Delta_bar_DF,s >= g_min J_b,48` but `<= 3 SE_eff,DF`; `FAIL` if `Delta_bar_DF,s < g_min J_b,48`. Label `GAIN_RESOLVED_ABOVE_MIN` = (`Delta_bar_DF,s - t SE_eff,DF >= g_min J_b,48`), reported, no effect.
- **D (drag non-increase, allowance 0):** the decision uses the point sign `Delta_bar_drag,s <= 0`; labels `PASS_RESOLVED` (`Delta_bar_drag,s + t SE_eff,drag <= 0`), `PASS_UNRESOLVED`, `VIOLATION_UNRESOLVED` (`0 < Delta_bar_drag,s <= t SE_eff,drag`), `VIOLATION_RESOLVED`; both violation forms block GAIN; `PASS_UNRESOLVED` is the expected label at the FID drag sigma_d (a GAIN may carry an unresolved drag constraint; XV-01 re-evaluates it on OpenFOAM, requirements §3b).
- **R3 (flow_48, Φ_48 ⊆ {13, 14}):** `R3_CONFIRMED` if `Delta_bar_DF,48 > 3 SE_eff,48` (floor 2.09e-4 N; |Φ_48| = 1 → `SE_eff,48 = floor`); `R3_SIGN_SPLIT` if `Delta_bar_DF,48 <= 0` (a point sign split is a fail, as #64); else `R3_UNRESOLVED`. Labels: `R3_RATIO_IN_BAND` (`Delta_bar_DF,48 / Delta_bar_DF,32(k13, k14) in [0.8, 1.25]`), `G48_ABOVE_MIN` (`Delta_bar_DF,48 >= g_min J_b,48`, with its margin), `R3_E2_EXCEEDED` (`|Delta_bar_DF,48 - Delta_bar_DF,32(k13, k14)| > 2 S_d J_b,48 = 1.807e-3 N`), `R3_DRAG_SAME_SIGN`. flow_48 not run (budget) or both kernels lost → `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`.
- **NC (G1 and F):** `z = |Delta_bar_roll - (-1.6476407624087992e-3)| / sqrt((3e-4 / sqrt(P))^2 + (8.534104568937632e-5)^2)`; CONSISTENT if `z <= 3.5` at both stages (G1: P <= 2; F: P <= 4); else `SCREEN01_INVALID`.
- **Determinism and drift:** the within-kernel `parent_repeat` must reproduce the parent's forces within the downforce floor 2.09e-4 N in g1k11 and f32a; a larger difference is `NONDETERMINISM` → `SCREEN01_INVALID` (the pairing assumption fails). The f32a parent run at phase 11 against g1k11's parent at phase 11: `GENERATION_DRIFT` if the difference exceeds the floor (label; pairs are within kernel).
- **Geometry:** no geometry check runs after a force; admissibility (incl. the chain check) was decided at P0 / before G2. No amendment may alter the identity or bytes of the finalist, R-best or M; a defect that would require it is `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`.

### 5.6 Control comparisons (confirmation phases; labels)

Planning projections, no outcome effect, handed to XV-01.
- `g_min` is applied exactly as registered; no additional floor and no raised target.

### 5.8 Outcome (evaluation order)
1. Minimum data: a verified G1 parent run at >= 1 phase and NC with >= 1 phase; else `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (cause `DATA_G1`).
2. NC not CONSISTENT (G1), or `NONDETERMINISM` in g1k11: `SCREEN01_INVALID`.
3. No finalist (§5.4): `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` if `search_complete = false` (cause `DATA_G1` / `DATA_REFINE` / `DATA_G2`); else `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN` (`NO_FEASIBLE_CANDIDATE` / `GAIN_BELOW_MIN_AT_SELECTION`; `POOL_LIMITED` reported).
4. Confirmation data: |Φ_s| < 3, flow_48 not measured, or F not completed (budget): `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (`DATA_F` / `BUDGET`).
5. NC not CONSISTENT (F), or `NONDETERMINISM` in f32a: `SCREEN01_INVALID`.
6. G `FAIL`; D `VIOLATION_RESOLVED` or `VIOLATION_UNRESOLVED`; `R3_SIGN_SPLIT`: `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN`, cause the first of `GAIN_BELOW_MIN`, `DRAG_VIOLATION`, `GRID_SIGN_SPLIT` that applies (all failing rules listed).
7. G `UNRESOLVED` or `R3_UNRESOLVED`: `SCREEN01_INCONCLUSIVE`.
8. G `PASS`, D point sign <= 0, `R3_CONFIRMED`: **`SCREEN01_GAIN`**.
Nothing in steps 3-8 is repaired from a force: no added phase, candidate, proposal or re-fit.

## 9. Freeze, re-run, amendment and stopping rules (fail-closed)
- Order: §2. Freezes 1, 2, 2.1 and 3 and the written-once outputs are committed, pushed and commented on #64 before the runs they determine.
- **`INFRA_LOSS` (mechanical):** a kernel ending with `ERROR` before its last run, or with no output, whose Kaggle log matches a frozen signature table (Julia install / `Pkg.instantiate` failure before any state, GPU identity mismatch in the harness probe, a Kaggle platform error page or `kaggle kernels status` error, a session kill without `DONE`/`ERROR.txt`; default = **not** infrastructure), or whose output fails harness `verify`. One identical rerun per kernel; the first valid output is authoritative; both are archived. A kernel lost again is declared to the analyzer (`--lost`); its runs are DATA_LOSS (§5.1, §5.9). A divergent run, an implausible value (bound §5.1) or a candidate the solver cannot handle is DATA_LOSS for that state-phase, never a reason to rerun.
- **Engineering defect found by a smoke before freeze 2 / 2.1 / 3:** fixed and re-frozen (amendment with diff and reason); rules, pools, designs and the identity/bytes of the finalist, R-best and M never change (a defect needing that → `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`).
- **Amendment after freeze 2 (`REGISTERED_AMENDED`):** only for a defect that prevents a pre-specified quantity, filed on #64 before any **verdict-relevant output** (a confirmation-stage or flow_48 force) is read, never touching a rule or a pool; the original outcome (or `INCOMPLETE`) is kept beside it. The G2 and confirmation campaign files are generated by the frozen builder (`screen01_build.py`) from `rs.json` / `finalist.json` and checked byte-equal at freeze 2.1 / 3.
- **Analyzer-defect protocol:** freeze 1 contains synthetic-table tests of every §5.8/§5.9 branch and the independent `screen01_check.py`; each written-once output is cross-checked before writing; after a write it stands and any discrepancy found later is reported beside it in a dated note.
- Written-once outputs: `rs.json`, `finalist.json`, `analysis.json`, `analysis.md`, `xv_handoff.json`; the analyzer re-derives each from the verified tables and the freezes and refuses on any mismatch.
- Stopping: the generations are finite (G1, G2, F); no stage is repeated; §4.3 caps and triggers; a second `INFRA_LOSS` of the same kernel is final.
