## 1. Question, hypothesis and claim ceiling
**Question (L1, roadmap M3b-1; #64).** Does a gradient-free, fixed-topology procedure — a quadratic response surface on the three strongest analytic modes of the v18 plate, a solver-free gated refinement of the surface's constrained optimum inside the registered geometry gates, and a finalist chosen by a fixed rule from the measured candidates — find, under drag non-increase, a WaterLily flow_32 paired downforce gain of at least `g_min` on fresh placement phases, with the same resolved sign on flow_48?

Claim ceiling: Re = 80 reduced-laminar WaterLily (Kaggle T4), one analytic parent (v18), three analytic wide modes (pitch, camber, reflex) with heave fixed at 0 and twist/sweep excluded, the box of §3.1 (camber extended to -29.55 mm, an amplitude with no FID fidelity evidence), in-plane placement phases only (every candidate carries an unphased z-lattice systematic that no phase averages and that the fresh-phase estimate does not remove; heave = 0 removes only its rigid part), the registered R0 width gate with its placement artefact, exploration and confirmation phases that are different deterministic points of the R2 torus (not independent draws). A `SCREEN01_GAIN` is a statement about **one finalist's paired same-phase differences against the parent inside the WaterLily solver family**; `g_min` is judged on flow_32 only, flow_48 contributes the resolved sign and labels. It is not independent verification (XV-01 #65 is), not an optimized-shape claim beyond L1, not physical downforce, not grid independence, not FSAE, not high Re. The finalist's expected OpenFOAM gain is a planning projection (§5.7), not a result.

Outcomes (§5.8): `SCREEN01_GAIN`, `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN` (with a cause), `SCREEN01_INCONCLUSIVE` (search complete, a rule unresolved by noise), `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (data loss or budget; includes a search that could not be completed), `SCREEN01_INVALID` (a chain control fails: NC or determinism). Registered secondary labels (no effect on the outcome): drag resolution, `GAIN_RESOLVED_ABOVE_MIN`, `R3_RATIO_IN_BAND`, `G48_ABOVE_MIN`, `R3_E2_EXCEEDED`, `R3_DRAG_SAME_SIGN`, `POOL_LIMITED`, `RS_FIT_{OK,POOR}`, `OPT_VS_RANDOM`, `OPT_VS_MANUAL`, `RANDOM_FEASIBLE_GAIN`, `AMPLITUDE_MISMATCH`, `K_MISMATCH`, `XV_MARGIN_{ADEQUATE,THIN}` (central and pessimistic), `XV_DRAG_MARGIN`, `GENERATION_DRIFT`.

## 3. Search space, pool and controls
### 3.1 Modes, box and normalisation
The subset is **fixed before any force: pitch, camber, reflex** (`v18_plate.UNITS` signs: pitch + nose down, camber + mid-chord up, reflex + trailing edge down). The choice uses visible values (§8): on flow_32 the single-mode downforce differences at A* are pitch 4.1e-2, reflex 1.5e-2, camber 1.3e-2, twist 1.6e-3, sweep about 1e-4 N (an 8x gap between the third and the fourth mode), so a measured ranking step would have a predictable result and the roadmap's "one mode at a time" screen is reduced to the six axial points of this subset (§14). **Heave is fixed at 0** (a SCREEN decision: pure heave failed FID tier B, the ride height is a vehicle setting, and a heave component would add the rigid part of the z-lattice systematic). Twist and sweep are excluded (null levers at their gate-clean amplitudes). Local/narrow modes stay excluded.

Box in normalised coordinates `a_m = u_m A*_m` with the MODE-01 v18 A* (pitch 3.1 deg, camber 19.7 mm, reflex 29.3 mm): `u_pitch in [-1, 1]`, **`u_camber in [-1.5, 1]`** (camber down to -29.55 mm; the MODE-01 ladder points 29.3 and 41.1 failed R0 at the native placement, so the extension is a region of lower admissible fraction, every point gated individually), `u_reflex in [-1, 1]`. Nothing outside the box is built. Interior and joint points are not MODE-01-tested; the FID fidelity evidence covers camber amplitudes 11.3 and 19.7 mm only, so a finalist in the extension rests on XV-01 for its fidelity (disclosed in the handoff).

### 3.2 Construction
A candidate is a dict `{mode: amplitude}` built by `scripts/fid01/fid01_designs.py` on the v18 parent: the modes are applied **jointly** to the plate parameters and `v18_plate.plate_phi` is evaluated once (an exact plate SDF); only active nodes change; the evaluated bytes are the harness state model `construct_state(parent, d / max|d|, 1000 max|d| mm, +1)`. State names are `fid01_designs.name(spec)` with the spec keys in sorted order; amplitudes are rounded to 1e-3 mm or deg.

### 3.6 Width rule and the disclosed selection bias of admissibility
Admissibility uses the registered R0 at the native placement, unchanged (GEOM-03 did not qualify R3). R0 fails about 40-60 % of sub-cell placements of this plate for a one-cell occupancy artefact (P0: 58 % of the joint 3-mode points in the unextended box, 45 % of the design pool in the rev. 1 box, 48 % of the random-control points evaluated). Consequences, stated: (i) the design, the refinement proposals and the finalist are admissible members, not the best points of the box; (ii) the refinement gates up to 36 points around the predicted optimum so that admissibility costs proposals, not the search direction; (iii) admissibility is placement-dependent and is not re-evaluated at the exploration or confirmation phases; (iv) no failure is repaired, re-sampled or re-phased. The same filter applies to R and M.

**Cap rule** (source review B4; the aggregate caps of revision 1 were 1.19-1.22x the slowest measured run for g1 and f48): the aggregate solver cap of a formal kernel is **1.5x its registered maximum run count x the per-run estimate, rounded up to 100 s** (about 1.45x the slowest measured flow_32 session mean, 1.4x the slowest flow_48 run); the kernel timeout is the cap + 3,300 s (the harness reserve, instantiate and probe allowances); the per-state cap is unchanged (900 / 6,000 s, at least 2.1x the slowest measured run). A kernel whose run count is below the registered maximum (an empty R-best, fewer proposals) keeps the registered cap. The estimates (and the §4.3 budget rule, which uses them) are unchanged. A cap trip is a kernel loss (§9: one identical rerun), never a reason to extend a cap after freeze 2.

Seven formal kernels; one phase per G1 kernel (a kernel lost twice costs one exploration phase); every pair (candidate and parent at the same phase) inside one kernel. The two G1 kernels and the two f48 kernels run concurrently. Formal estimate 61,095 s = **17.0 GPU h**, plus four smokes (G1 campaign `screen01`, G2 campaign `screen01_g2`, flow_32 confirmation campaign `screen01_f`, flow_48 campaign `screen01_f48`; about 1.45 h) = about 18.4 GPU h; every kernel timeout is at most 27,600 s = 7.7 h, inside the 12 h session limit. Campaign files (engineering, fixed by the harness): the harness binds each campaign file's SHA-256 into its freeze and into every run identity, so a campaign is never extended after its prerun freeze. The proposals therefore form their own campaign `screen01_g2` (freeze 2.1). **Registered smoke kernels** (one per campaign; the smoke table bound by the following freeze must verify exactly this kernel, else the freeze is refused): `screen01` → `g1k11` (its first run, the parent at phase 11), `screen01_g2` → `g2` (the parent at phase 11), `screen01_f` → `f32smk`, `screen01_f48` → `f48smk`. The confirmation smokes run dedicated smoke-only kernels that are never pushed as formal kernels and are not in the estimate: `f32smk` in `screen01_f` (the parent alone at phase 20, flow_32) and `f48smk` in `screen01_f48` (the parent alone at phase 20, flow_48); their caps (per-state 890 / 5,990 s, aggregate the same, timeout 4,200 / 9,300 s) are engineering numbers chosen so that the harness's default smoke-kernel choice (the smallest solver length, then the smallest per-state cap) selects the smoke-only kernel even without `--kernel`; the smoke command is `scripts/harness.py smoke CAMPAIGN --source-commit X --kernel K` with the registered kernel in every case. A formal kernel directory is pushed one by one by name; no glob over `infra/kaggle/kernel_screen01_*` (it would include the rendered smoke-only kernels). Run names are `<state>__k<phase>`; the parent repeat is the state `v18__parent__repeat` (the parent's bytes under its own name); in f32a the phase-11 replay and then the repeat follow the phase-13 and phase-14 blocks. The harness prerun freeze and the per-kernel verification both go through `scripts/fid01/fid01_harness.py` (`freeze`, `verify`; FID amendment 2: the owner's listing carries an unaddressable "[Private Notebook]" row that the stock identity-free check fails closed on).

### 4.3 Budget, quota, queue
Cap **24 GPU h** (86,400 s) of formal session wall (Kaggle session wall from each kernel log; smokes excluded, kernel reruns (§9) included). Before every push after G1: `T = sum(session wall of finished formal kernels) + sum(estimates of every unfinished formal kernel, reruns included)`, written once by `analyze_screen01.py budget --out` as a budget record; the record of the G2 push is bound by freeze 2.1, that of the first F push by freeze 3, and the records of the later F pushes (after f48k13 / f48k14 finish) are kept under `wl/` and listed in `SHA256SUMS`. If `T > 86,400 s` the pushes stop and the decision goes to the user (not an automatic rule change); if the study is not resumed, the kernels never pushed are declared `--not-pushed KERNEL=BUDGET` citing the stop record, and the outcome is `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (cause `BUDGET`, §5.8 step 4). One G1 rerun (4.6 h) fits inside the cap; two do not. Push order in F: f48k13 and f48k14 first, then f32a, f32b. If the weekly quota (30 GPU h) stops a push: pause, resume in order. **Backend trigger** (STATE.md): a kernel waiting more than 24 h in the queue, or the quota blocking the remaining kernels for more than 7 days, stops the pushes and is reported; finished kernels stay valid.

### 4.4 Blinding
While kernels run only statuses, session times and `verify` pass/fail are read (per-kernel verify tables to files, stdout discarded). Forces are read only by the written-once steps (`rs` after G1, `finalist` after G2, `main` after F), each after all kernels of its generation are verified or declared lost. The confirmation-stage states are fixed in freeze 3 before any confirmation run.

## 5. Rules (computed by the frozen `scripts/screen01/analyze_screen01.py`, cross-checked by `screen01_check.py`)
Responses: downforce (objective) and drag (constraint), N, as the harness state-force tables report them. Differences are candidate minus parent at the same phase in the same kernel.

### 5.1 Estimators and data loss
- For a state s and a phase set Φ (the phases common to s and the parent in the stage's kernels, after data loss): `Delta_bar = mean_k(F_s,k - F_parent,k)`, `sigma_d` (|Φ| - 1 dof) as M2 (`analyze_unc01.stats`); `SE_eff = max(sigma_d, floor) / sqrt(|Φ|)` with the M2 floors `0.00020904201605451784 N` (downforce) and `0.000136408347135453 N` (drag); with |Φ| = 1, `SE_eff = floor`. `t = t_{0.95, |Φ|-1}` where a t is used (labels only). Gains are reported as `I = Delta_bar / J_b,48`.
- **DATA_LOSS (state-phase):** a run whose force is non-finite, whose job exceeded its per-state cap or did not finish, whose harness verify failed, or whose `|F - F_parent,k| > 0.25 |F_parent,k|` for either response (frozen implausibility bound; the largest registered difference is 15 %) is excluded from every estimator and counted in §5.9. A lost kernel loses every state-phase in it. **Unit of loss under harness v1.1 (source review R3/B3):** the runner stops at the first state that diverges, exceeds its cap or does not finish, and harness verify is all-or-nothing per kernel, so each of the first three events voids the whole kernel (no verified table; §9 kernel loss: one identical rerun, then declared lost), and the only state-phase loss that can occur on a verified table is the implausibility bound. The analyzer and the checker nevertheless apply every clause to every run row of a table (`ok = false`, non-finite), so that a future harness that reports per-state failures needs no rule change. The forces are read as the harness-verified table reports them (the table, whose per-run CSV and summary hashes the analyzer re-checks against the kernel files, is the trust anchor; no force is recomputed from a CSV).
- Exploration estimates use Φ ⊆ {11, 12}; confirmation estimates use Φ ⊆ {13, 14, 15, 16} (flow_32) and {13, 14} (flow_48). They are never pooled.

### 5.2 Response surface and the refinement step (`rs`, written once after G1)
On the design points with data (6 axial + 9 design; a point is dropped only if both its phases are lost) fit by ordinary least squares, for downforce `y` and drag `z`, the no-intercept full quadratic `f(u) = b'u + u'Qu` (9 coefficients; `f(0) = 0` exactly because a difference vanishes at the parent). Report coefficients, residual SD, the lack-of-fit ratio against the pooled per-point `SE_eff` (`RS_FIT_POOR` if the residual SD exceeds 3x the pooled SE_eff; descriptive), and the matrix rank and condition number; if rank < 9 or cond > 20 → `RS_NOT_FIT` (no proposals; `search_complete = false` if a loss caused it). Report the constrained optimum over the box grid (81 points per axis), `u*_1`, `y*_1 = max ŷ s.t. ẑ <= -m_D`, and `POOL_LIMITED_PRE` = (`y*_1 >= g_min J_b,48`).

**Refinement (`screen01_pool.propose`, deterministic; parameters frozen; details §5.11.4-5.11.5):** `m_D = 1.5e-3 N`; targets `tau in {1, 1.5, 2}` with the 0-based target index `i = 0, 1, 2`. For each target the constrained argmax `u*_tau` over the 81^3 box grid (`linspace(lo, hi, 81)` per axis) with `ẑ <= -tau m_D` (ties → the first grid point in lexicographic (pitch, camber, reflex) order); `ŷ*_tau = ŷ(u*_tau)`. A target with no feasible grid point, or with `ŷ*_tau <= 0`, contributes nothing (recorded; `u*_tau`, `ŷ*_tau`, `ẑ*_tau` are still reported). The candidate list of a target = `u*_tau` first, then the 256 points `u = clip(u*_tau + 0.25 (2x - 1), lo, hi)` of the scrambled Sobol sequence `x` (`scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol(d = 3, scramble = True, seed = 20261032 + i).random(256)`; a max-norm ball of radius 0.25 u, clipped to the box) kept if `ẑ <= -tau m_D` and `ŷ >= 0.9 ŷ*_tau`, in `ŷ`-descending order (stable); a list holds each state name (the §3.2 identity: amplitudes rounded to 1e-3, zero amplitudes dropped, sorted keys) once (its first occurrence), and the names of the states registered at freeze 1 (axial, design, M, ref, R) are excluded. The three lists are interleaved round-robin (position 0 of each list in target order, then position 1, ...) into one queue; a name an earlier list already queued is skipped, so every point is gated once and belongs to the target that queued it first. The queue is gated (full admissibility, §3.3) in rounds of 4 points in queue order, stopping after the round in which every target with at least one queued point has >= 2 admissible points of its own, or when 36 points were gated. **Proposals**: per target in target order its admissible point with the largest `ŷ`, then the remaining admissible points by `ŷ` (ties → the point gated earlier), at most 4. A target with no queued point or no admissible point contributes nothing (recorded); the slots it leaves empty are empty for lack of candidates, not for loss (`search_complete`, §5.4). The whole gating record (every point, its predictions and gate results) is written in `rs.json`; proposals are new states (direction raws + hashes written into the G2 campaign `screen01_g2` by the frozen builder, freeze 2.1). No continuous argmax is run as a geometry, no extrapolation outside the box, nothing is chosen by a force.

### 5.3 G2
The proposals are run at phases 11 and 12 with the parent. No further proposals, no second round.

### 5.4 Finalist, R-best (`finalist`, written once after G2)
Candidate set `C_opt` = the 6 axial states, the 9 design points and the proposals (`K_opt = |C_opt| <= 19`). **Feasible** = `Delta_bar_drag <= 0` (exploration point estimate; the registered precedent `DRAG_ALLOWANCE_N = 0.0`). **Finalist** = the feasible candidate with the largest exploration `Delta_bar_DF`; ties (exact equality) → registered order (axial in mode order + before -, design points in freeze order, proposals in rank order). **Futility**: if `C_opt` has no feasible member, or the finalist's `Delta_bar_DF < g_min J_b,48 - 3 SE_eff` (the FID tier convention for resolution; it cannot reach §5.5 G), there is no finalist and F is not pushed; the outcome is `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN` (cause `NO_FEASIBLE_CANDIDATE` / `GAIN_BELOW_MIN_AT_SELECTION`) if `search_complete`, else `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (§5.9). **`search_complete`** = every search state (the parent, the 6 axial and the 9 design states) has both exploration phases after DATA_LOSS, `rs` was fit, every proposal slot is either filled or empty for lack of candidates (a target with no feasible grid point, no predicted gain, no queued or no admissible point; not for loss), and every proposal has both phases; losses of R, M, ref, NC or the parent repeat do not enter it (§5.9, §5.11.6). **`POOL_LIMITED`** = `POOL_LIMITED_PRE` and no feasible candidate's exploration `Delta_bar_DF >= g_min J_b,48` (the surface predicted a feasible gain the evaluated candidates did not reach; descriptive, carried to the result). **R-best** = the same rule over the 19 random states; none feasible → R-best empty (`RANDOM_NO_FEASIBLE`). Output: both selections, `K_opt`, `K_rand`, every candidate's exploration estimates, the confirmation run lists, and the **G1 chain-control report** `g1_controls` (the §5.5 NC verdict at G1 and the g1k11 determinism verdict, computed exactly as `main` computes them for §5.8 step 2, cross-checked; also written by `rs`). If `g1_controls` reports `NC_G1` NOT_CONSISTENT or `NONDETERMINISM_G1K11`, the outcome is already `SCREEN01_INVALID` at step 2 whatever G2 or F would show: the frozen builder refuses to generate the G2 and confirmation campaigns, nothing further is pushed, the un-pushed kernels are declared `--not-pushed KERNEL=INVALID_G1` (§5.11.19), and `main` writes the INVALID outcome from the measured tables (about 11 GPU h are not spent on a decided outcome; no rule and no threshold changes).

### 5.5 Confirmation decision (`main`, written once after F)
For the finalist f (and, descriptively, R-best and M) with the confirmation flow_32 phases Φ_s (|Φ_s| >= 3 required for f, else `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`):
- **G (gain, the FID tier form; g_min exactly as registered):** `PASS` if `Delta_bar_DF,s >= g_min J_b,48` and `Delta_bar_DF,s > 3 SE_eff,DF`; `UNRESOLVED` if `Delta_bar_DF,s >= g_min J_b,48` but `<= 3 SE_eff,DF`; `FAIL` if `Delta_bar_DF,s < g_min J_b,48`. Label `GAIN_RESOLVED_ABOVE_MIN` = (`Delta_bar_DF,s - t SE_eff,DF >= g_min J_b,48`), reported, no effect.
- **D (drag non-increase, allowance 0):** the decision uses the point sign `Delta_bar_drag,s <= 0`; labels `PASS_RESOLVED` (`Delta_bar_drag,s + t SE_eff,drag <= 0`), `PASS_UNRESOLVED`, `VIOLATION_UNRESOLVED` (`0 < Delta_bar_drag,s <= t SE_eff,drag`), `VIOLATION_RESOLVED`; both violation forms block GAIN; `PASS_UNRESOLVED` is the expected label at the FID drag sigma_d (a GAIN may carry an unresolved drag constraint; XV-01 re-evaluates it on OpenFOAM, requirements §3b).
- **R3 (flow_48, Φ_48 ⊆ {13, 14}):** `R3_CONFIRMED` if `Delta_bar_DF,48 > 3 SE_eff,48` (floor 2.09e-4 N; |Φ_48| = 1 → `SE_eff,48 = floor`); `R3_SIGN_SPLIT` if `Delta_bar_DF,48 <= 0` (a point sign split is a fail, as #64); else `R3_UNRESOLVED`. Labels: `R3_RATIO_IN_BAND` (`Delta_bar_DF,48 / Delta_bar_DF,32(k13, k14) in [0.8, 1.25]`), `G48_ABOVE_MIN` (`Delta_bar_DF,48 >= g_min J_b,48`, with its margin), `R3_E2_EXCEEDED` (`|Delta_bar_DF,48 - Delta_bar_DF,32(k13, k14)| > 2 S_d J_b,48 = 1.807e-3 N`), `R3_DRAG_SAME_SIGN`. flow_48 not run (budget) or both kernels lost → `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`.
- **NC (G1 and F):** `z = |Delta_bar_roll - (-1.6476407624087992e-3)| / sqrt((3e-4 / sqrt(P))^2 + (8.534104568937632e-5)^2)`; CONSISTENT if `z <= 3.5` at both stages (G1: P <= 2; F: P <= 4); else `SCREEN01_INVALID`.
- **Determinism and drift:** the within-kernel `parent_repeat` must reproduce the parent's forces within the downforce floor 2.09e-4 N in g1k11 and f32a; a larger difference is `NONDETERMINISM` → `SCREEN01_INVALID` (the pairing assumption fails). The f32a parent run at phase 11 against g1k11's parent at phase 11: `GENERATION_DRIFT` if the difference exceeds the floor (label; pairs are within kernel).
- **Geometry:** no geometry check runs after a force; admissibility (incl. the chain check) was decided at P0 / before G2. No amendment may alter the identity or bytes of the finalist, R-best or M; a defect that would require it is `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE`.

### 5.6 Control comparisons (confirmation phases; labels)
Per confirmation phase k, `e_k = Delta_DF,f,k - Delta_DF,R-best,k`; `OPT_BEATS_RANDOM` if `mean(e) - t SE(e) > 0`, `RANDOM_BEATS_OPT` if `mean(e) + t SE(e) < 0`, else `OPT_RANDOM_UNRESOLVED`; `RANDOM_NO_FEASIBLE` if R-best is empty; `K_MISMATCH` if `K_opt != K_rand`. The same with M (`OPT_VS_MANUAL`; `MANUAL_NONE` if M is empty). `RANDOM_FEASIBLE_GAIN` if R-best itself passes G, D and R3. The exploration-phase versions and the `|u|_inf` distributions (`AMPLITUDE_MISMATCH`) are reported beside them. One draw each; descriptive.

### 5.7 Selection-bias statement (registered disclosure, computed by the analyzer)
- Placement-noise bias: `Delta_bar_DF,sel - Delta_bar_DF,s` of the finalist with its SE (the decision never uses the selection-phase estimate; the two phase sets are deterministic R2 points, so independence is assumed, not shown).
- WaterLily-to-OpenFOAM bias (not removable here): `b_K = E[max of K_opt standard normals] x sigma_disc` with the **primary `sigma_disc = 5.61e-4 N`** (the FID tier-A WaterLily-to-V1 residual SD about the through-origin slope 0.655; retention SD 0.058 x 1e-2 N gives the same order) and the **upper bound 1.4474e-3 N** (the FID G3-set residual SD, an OpenFOAM-internal V3-on-V1 quantity); `E[max_19] = 1.844` (computed by the analyzer for the actual K). The selection acts on WaterLily values and the retention already attenuates the OpenFOAM value, so `b_K` partly double-counts and the effective number of near-top competitors is smaller than K; both make the statement conservative. **Expected OpenFOAM gain** at level V1 (the FID G rules' level): central `g_XV,exp = 0.655 (I_s - b_K / J_b,48)` with the primary `sigma_disc`, pessimistic `0.57 (I_s - b_K,upper / J_b,48)`; `XV_MARGIN_ADEQUATE / THIN` for each against the floor `0.012674959854238867` (at K = 19 the central boundary is `I_s = 2.32 %`, the pessimistic `3.23 %`). `XV_DRAG_MARGIN`: the confirmation drag difference with its interval, and the statement that the finalist's drag neutrality relies on the OpenFOAM drag retention of camber (untested in FID; tested 0.58-0.87 for pitch, reflex and combinations). Planning projections, no outcome effect, handed to XV-01.
- `g_min` is applied exactly as registered; no additional floor and no raised target.

### 5.8 Outcome (evaluation order)
1. Minimum data: a verified G1 parent run at >= 1 phase and NC with >= 1 phase; else `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (cause `DATA_G1`).
2. NC not CONSISTENT (G1), or `NONDETERMINISM` in g1k11: `SCREEN01_INVALID`.
3. No finalist (§5.4): `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` if `search_complete = false` (cause `DATA_G1` / `DATA_REFINE` / `DATA_G2`); else `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN` (`NO_FEASIBLE_CANDIDATE` / `GAIN_BELOW_MIN_AT_SELECTION`; `POOL_LIMITED` reported).
4. Confirmation data: F not completed (a confirmation kernel declared `--not-pushed KERNEL=BUDGET`; evaluated first, whatever the other confirmation data show), |Φ_s| < 3, flow_48 not measured, or NC without an F phase: `SCREEN01_INCOMPLETE` (`BUDGET` / `DATA_F`, §5.11.15).
5. NC not CONSISTENT (F), or `NONDETERMINISM` in f32a: `SCREEN01_INVALID`.
6. G `FAIL`; D `VIOLATION_RESOLVED` or `VIOLATION_UNRESOLVED`; `R3_SIGN_SPLIT`: `SCREEN01_NO_GAIN`, cause the first of `GAIN_BELOW_MIN`, `DRAG_VIOLATION`, `GRID_SIGN_SPLIT` that applies (all failing rules listed).
7. G `UNRESOLVED` or `R3_UNRESOLVED`: `SCREEN01_INCONCLUSIVE`.
8. G `PASS`, D point sign <= 0, `R3_CONFIRMED`: **`SCREEN01_GAIN`**.
Nothing in steps 3-8 is repaired from a force: no added phase, candidate, proposal or re-fit.

### 5.11 Rule details fixed before freeze 1 (cross-check reconciliation of 2026-10-31)
Two independent implementations of §5 (`analyze_screen01.py`, `screen01_check.py`) were cross-checked on synthetic tables before freeze 1; where the text was silent, ambiguous or disagreed with `screen01_pool.propose`, the registrant decided as follows (reasons: `docs/evidence/screen01_2026_10_30/crosscheck_reconciliation.md`). Each item refines the section it names. None changes `g_min`, the drag allowance, the pools, the design, M or the random set; (T) marks a reading stricter than one implementation had, (R) a replaced default; the rest confirm what both implementations did.
1. (§5.1) DATA_LOSS applies uniformly to every run of every state (the parent repeat, NC, R, M and ref included): `ok = false` (verify failed, per-state cap exceeded, not finished), a non-finite force, or `|F - F_parent,k| > 0.25 |F_parent,k|` for either response against the parent's finite `ok` run of the same kernel group and phase. A run with no valid parent run at its phase enters no estimator and is not listed; runs missing because a kernel was declared lost are not listed individually (the kernel is). 2. (§5.1) `sigma_d` = sample SD (|Φ| - 1 dof); |Φ| = 1 → `sigma_d` null and `SE_eff` = floor. `t = t_{0.95, |Φ|-1}` (one-sided quantile); a t-based label with |Φ| = 1 is null, not computed.
3.
