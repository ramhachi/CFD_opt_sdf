## Diagnosis of the failing gate (descriptive, post-run, changes no verdict)

| State | Failing | tie_plateau | kink | other |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| baseline | 106 | 25 | 63 | 18 |
| parent | 154 | 0 | 149 | 5 |

`kink`: opposite one-sided slopes of magnitude about 1 along an axis, i.e. a node on a ridge of an accurate distance field where the solid is one
node thick in that axis (99/106 and 153/154 failing nodes are solid). The distance is correct but the normal is undefined there, so the
shape-derivative normal velocity `-δφ/|∇φ|` is ill-conditioned. **A reinitialization cannot remove a kink** (an exact SDF keeps it); only a finer
representation or thicker geometry can. `tie_plateau`: exact-zero ties from genesis (baseline only). The parent's larger kink count follows the
un-reinitialized LOWDIM step. Consequence: the registered A4 rule will fail on any state with one-node-thick edges, whatever #28 does; before #30
the rule needs a decision (keep it as a hard gate and change the representation, or a new profile that separates kinks from degeneracies).
That decision is open and is not made here.
