## Facts used (exploratory unless stated)

- **Count radius.** `_corner_mean` moves by at most the max-norm change of phi. Baseline min \|centre\| 3.125 mm (105 cells under 3.125 mm);
  parent min solid \|centre\| 2.407 mm (4 cells under 2.5 mm), min fluid centre 3.228 mm. N can only grow when a fluid cell turns solid, so
  N <= 8272 holds for any state within 3.228 mm (max norm) of the parent, and N = 8272 exactly within 2.407 mm, up to Float32 rounding
  (about 7e-4 mm analytic bound; margin about 1.15 mm at 1.25 mm steps). First observed count change along retained directions from the
  parent: 2.8 to 7 mm; N > 8272 first at about 3.7 mm or more. The parent radius shrank from 3.125 to 2.407 mm after one 1.25 mm step: it must
  be recomputed at every parent.
- **Builder.** The eight recorded LOWDIM-04A states re-derive bit-exactly (8 of 8 Fortran-order phi SHA matches) from the parent with
  `step01_states.perturb`, closing the "recorded, not re-derived" caveat of #55 for those states. P22-v2 itself is not amended.
- **Retained ladder** (LOWDIM-03, proposal direction, both grids): flow_24 even part -4.1e-5, -1.50e-4, -5.95e-4 N (clean s² law), odd
  part linear in s to 2% against the GRID-01 secant; flow_32 drag even parts do not follow s². Cost per state: flow_24 about 176 s,
  flow_32 about 408 s (a nine-state kernel fits the 900 s per-state timeout).
- **Objective hierarchy.** LOWDIM is the insurance track. #30 depends on #25, #26, #27, #28, #29; #51 is on no gate's critical path. The
  accepted gain (+2.1e-4 N, 0.06%) is below the Stage V resolution (OpenFOAM 3-SE about 2.2e-4 N). A second iteration is predicted at about 1e-4 N.

### Cycle 1: Stage A (#51), one preregistration, then stop

Preregistered choices (every one is frozen before submission):

- **Authorization and blindness.** Cite the authorization above. List `known_before_run` (STEP-01, LOWDIM-01, LOWDIM-02A, GRID-01,
  LOWDIM-03, P22, the exploratory fits): the registration is explicitly non-blind. Reviewers get frozen sources and paths, never excerpts of the new run.
- **States.** Per grid, parent first, then D0, D1, D2, P1 at +-1.25 mm: 9 states, 18 runs, two Kaggle T4 kernels. Built with the production
  `construct_state` and asserted byte-equal to `step01_states.perturb`; the realized float32 `maximum_pointwise_change_m` is frozen.
- **Identity table.** For every state four named hashes: `phi_fortran_order_sha256`, `phi_c_order_sha256`, `state_sha256`, `npz_sha256`; each
  consumer pinned to one (runner and device round trip: Fortran; P22-v2 evaluator and reference: C). A test fails if a field is compared in the wrong order.
- **Parent gate.** The fresh parent runs must be byte-identical to the retained LOWDIM-03 `prop__s1.25mm` force CSVs
  (`docs/evidence/lowdim03_dual_grid_primal_2026_10_10/failed_attempt1/kernel_output/lowdim03_{a,b}/states/lowdim03__prop__s1.25mm/`, whose integrity
  failure was analyzer-only, Amendment 1) before any perturbation runs; a mismatch is `LOWDIM04A_INCOMPLETE` with no `--write`, repair or resubmission.
- **Pre-CFD checks** on the exact Float32 bytes: the five existing geometry gates (base frozen: canonical v17 baseline, as in all LOWDIM gates) and the
  hard count N = 8272 via the production evaluator. A failure is a pre-freeze blocker; a runtime mismatch is INCOMPLETE. There is no in-run
  "geometry unsupported" branch for an arithmetically impossible event.
- **Volume rows.** Hard count: constant on the retained lattice (lemma with rounding terms), asserted per state. Smooth linearisation and the
  continuum zero-set volume (with centered secants and the signed first-order change of the solved direction in percent and in the D0 coefficient)
  are reported for every outcome. Sentence frozen: "volume feasibility here means the lattice count N <= 8272, exactly satisfied by construction; it
  constrains no physical or sub-voxel volume".
- **Model and subproblem.** Centered secants at s = 1.25 mm per grid and force, plus the descriptive odd and even parts. Normalization: l2 on
  coefficients with the max-norm rescale (the certified machinery), with the exact max-norm LP as a reported cross-check (scipy availability
  verified first). Floors in newtons on the predicted 1.25 mm step gain (`3e-5 N`, `m` computed from the actual arrays), `epsilon = 3e-5/(2 s) = 0.012 N/m`.
- **Verdict.** FOUND only if both the raw and the L1-robust problems pass (as in GRID-01); the robust family gates drag. Unresolved components do
  not trigger INCOMPLETE; their count and identity are reported; flow_32 drag below the floor is "unresolved, active at zero within the nominal floor".
  Terminals: `LOCAL_FEASIBLE_CONE_FOUND`, `LOCAL_FEASIBLE_CONE_NOT_FOUND`, `LOWDIM04A_INCOMPLETE`. One grid incomplete, a non-finite run, or any integrity
  failure is INCOMPLETE; no partial analysis is written.
- **Claims.** May say: responses measured; secant values; under the stated linear model a vector with the stated t* exists or not; comparison with GRID-01.
  May not say: downforce improves (the linear model overpredicted by about 40% at the last step), drag nonincrease, feasibility without "lattice-count only",
  grid convergence, #26 resolved, optimizer or second-iteration progress. The words parent, trust region and second iteration are experiment labels.
  All qualification flags false, `selected_delta=null`, `shape_update_allowed=false`, reinitialization none. No Stage B text is part of Stage A.
- **Code placement.** New files only: `scripts/*lowdim04a*.py`, `tests/test_lowdim04a_*.py`; frozen modules imported read-only; nothing under `src/cfd_sdf`
  or `julia/`. Source bound by commit tree hash and pushed to a remote branch (the runner fetches it). Manifests list tracked, non-ignored names only
  (narrow `.gitignore` negation or `git add -f`, plus a gate that every `SHA256SUMS` entry exists and is in `git ls-files`). No living document is hashed.
- **Dry runs before the freeze** (no GPU): the rendered runner stopped after state generation checking every phi SHA in both byte orders; the new
  `check_kernel` replayed over the retained real LOWDIM-03 output tree with only the expected hashes swapped; a tracked-versus-manifest check of a
  clean download; every identity check counted against the expected totals (the SRCMAP-03a lesson: 125 versus 128).
- **Process.** Independent adversarial review of the final bytes (read-only, no new-run excerpts), freeze, push, submit both kernels, analyzer `--check`
  then exactly one `--write`, independent post-run review, record, full pytest compared with the known 48 failures, `compileall`, `git diff --check`.

## Known limitations carried forward

Reviewers are subagents. The registration is non-blind. flow_32 response jitter is unmeasured beyond the retained ladder. The lemma and the radius cover the
Float32 phi artifact on the v17 lattice only. The even part is negative everywhere (cause untested: physical curvature, a baseline near an extremum, or loss of the
signed-distance property); it matters before any multi-step loop.

## Non-goals

No basis expansion, curvature model, reinitialization, new volume contract, signer qualification, production change, or flag change in these cycles.
